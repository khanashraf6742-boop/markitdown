economy kya hai?What is an economy · scarcity & choice

§
paribhashaeconomy ek aisa system hai jismein desh utpadan (produce), vitaran (distribute) aur upbhog (consume) — teeno kaarya goods aur services (goods & services) ka karta hai.

economy ki concept ka mool simit resources (limited resources) arthat abhav / kami (scarcity) hai — kyunki resources simit hain, isliye tay karna padta hai ki kis cheez ko praathmikta di jaaye.

kya utpadan karein
What to produce
kaise utpadan karein
How to produce
kiske liye utpadan karein
For whom to produce

yahi teen question “kendriya samasya (central problem)” hain — inhi ka uttar economy ki sanrachna tay karta hai.

vargikaran ka aadhar

economy ka vargikaran samanyatah is aadhar par hota hai — utpadan ke sadhanon ka swamitva (ownership of means of production) evam unmein government ki bhoomika.

  • utpadan ke sadhan = bhoomi, shram, poonji aur udyam (Land, Labour, Capital, Enterprise).
  • question ye: ye sadhan kiske pass hain — niji vyakti, rajya (State), ya dono?
  • doosra question: government kitni sakriya bhoomika nibhati hai — niyantran/kalyanakari hastakshep ya keval niyam-nirdharan?
i
pariksha sanket“economy kya hai” — ye seedhe question shayad hi aaye, par scarcity → choice → central problems aur ownership-based classification par aadharit question baar-baar bante hain.

04

economy ke prakaarCapitalist · Socialist · Mixed · Gandhian

kram yaad rakhein: poonjivadi → samajvadi → mishrit → Gandhivadi/samajvadi. pehle teen “swamitva” ke aadhar par, chautha “decentralisation” ke aadhar par.

1. poonjivadi economy (Capitalist Economy)

  • utpadan ke sadhan niji swamitva mein — badi companies ke pass.
  • labh-unmukh (profit oriented) model; bazaar ki shaktiyan (market forces) keemat tay karti hain.
  • government ki bhoomika nyoonatam/nishkriya — inflation niyantran jaise sakriya hastakshep nahi.
  • udaharan: USA.

2. samajvadi economy (Socialist Economy)

  • sadhanon par rajya (State) ka swamitva; economic shakti centralised (centralized).
  • economic nirnay jan-kalyan ke liye — munafa sarvopari nahi.
  • udaharan: yadi bazaar mein vastuein itni mahangi ho jaayein ki garib varg khareed na sake, to government hastakshep kar sasta/sulabh banati hai — yahi samajvadi vyavastha ki pehchan.

3. mishrit economy (Mixed Economy)

  • poonjivad + samajvad ka mel — niji (private) aur sarvajanik (public) dono sector saath-saath.
  • Bharat mishrit economy ka udaharan hai — ek or Tata, Ambani jaisi niji companies; doosri or PSU (sarvajanik sector).
  • saath hi kalyanakari (samajvadi) tatva: National Food Security Act (NFSA) ke antargat lagbhag 80 crore logon ko riyayati rate (subsidised rate) par khadyann.
  • sarvajanik sector ka sanchalan rajya karta hai, niji sector ka vyakti/niji body.
!
Trap — “State” ka arthyahan “State” ka matlab Haryana/Punjab/UP jaisa rajya nahi, balki Constitution ke Article 12 mein paribhashit “rajya” hai — jismein government, karyapalika, vidhayika aadi aate hain.

4. Gandhivadi samajvad (Gandhian Socialism)

  • aadhar: utpadak sansadhanon ka decentralisation (decentralization of productive resources) — resources Janata mein bante jaayein, kuch logon mein kendrit na hon.
  • jor swarozgar (self-employment), self-reliance (self-reliance) aur sthaniya/gram-stariy utpadan par.
  • vyavaharik sanket: pichhle kuch varshon mein government ka self-reliance par focus isi vichar ki pratidhvani hai (jaise self-reliant Bharat).

developing economy ke lakshan (Features of Developing Economy)

  • vyavsay mukhyatah agriculture — par GDP mein yogdan kam, nirbharta zyada.
  • prachhann unemployment (Disguised unemployment) — ek vyakti ka kaam chaar log kar rahe hain; teen hata dein to bhi utpadan par fark nahi.
  • human capital ki nimn quality — kaushal, education aur health ka abhav.
  • industrial aur takniki pichhadapan, mishrit economy, import par zyada nirbharta (import > export), nirakshatra.
✎

Trapunderdeveloped aur developing ke lakshan lagbhag saman dikhate hain — dono mein bhram na karein. pehchan: question mein “Bharat” poocha jaaye → developing; kisi samanya/saiddhantik economy ki baat ho → underdeveloped ke lakshan chunein.
