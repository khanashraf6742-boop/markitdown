## Min 00–07 — Rule 12: travel entitlement, trains, road/private vehicle

Lecture TA rules se start hoti hai. Simple line: **LTC ka travel entitlement TA entitlement jaisa hai**, jo Ministry of Finance OM **13.07.2017** se aaya. Lekin ek important exception hai:

> **Level 6 to Level 8** ka **air entitlement sirf TA ke liye** hai, **LTC ke liye nahi**. LTC mein unka rail entitlement **AC 2 Tier** tak hi rahega. Air LTC generally **Level 9 / usse upar** se start hoti hai. [S1 Rule 12; lecture min 1–2]

### Premium / dynamic-fare trains

- **Rajdhani / Shatabdi / Duronto** allowed hain, apni entitled class mein.
- Unka **flexi / dynamic fare** reimburse hota hai. [S1 Rule 12 note 4(i)]
- Lekin agar **non-air-entitled** employee air se gaya aur claim entitled Rajdhani/Shatabdi/Duronto class ke fare par kare, to **dynamic fare component add nahi** hoga — base fare tak cap. [S1]
- **Tatkal / Suvidha / premium trains** ka surcharge apne aap nahi milta; reimbursement entitled class / actual, jo kam ho, tak. [Lecture min 2–3; S1]

> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** **Tejas Express, Vande Bharat Express, Humsafar Express** bhi LTC par allowed hain, **14.01.2025** se. Table: Level 12+ → Executive / AC 1st; Level 6–11 → Chair Car / AC 2nd; Level 5 and below → Chair Car / AC 3rd. [S5] [S2 Q33]

### Road / private vehicle / last-mile

Lecture min 3–7 ka rule:

- HQ se nearest **airport / railway station / bus terminal** tak authorised mode; aage declared place of visit tak private/own arrangement.
- Agar us stretch par **public transport available** hai → reimbursement **otherwise entitled public transport fare**, shortest direct route. [S1] [S2 Q31]
- Agar **public transport hi nahi** hai → self-certification par **max 200 km total / 100 km each side**, “journey on transfer” entitlement ke hisaab se. Usse aage kharch employee ka. [S1] [S2 Q31]
- Taxi / auto sirf un jagahon par jahan rail/road connectivity nahi, aur vehicle State Govt / transport authority se **regular point-to-point public carrier** approve ho. [S2 Q29]
- Disability ke case mein own/hired taxi allowed, with medical certificate, undertaking, aur cap = entitled rail/air shortest-route fare. [S2 Q30]

> ❌ **Removed as obsolete:** lecture ka purana “Air India / LTC-80 / national carrier” framing. Air India ab Government PSU nahi rahi; LTC-80 scheme **exist nahi karti**. [S2 Q48] [S3]

## Min 07–16 — Air booking: ATAs, Best Available Fare, packages

### Three Authorized Travel Agents (ATAs)

1. **Balmer Lawrie & Company Limited (BLCL)**
2. **Ashok Travels & Tours (ATT)**
3. **IRCTC**

2022 OM ke baad LTC air ticket inhin teen se lene the; 2023 OM aur 2025 Guidelines ne isme refinement kiya. [S1] [S3 enclosure]

| Rule | Current position |
|---|---|
| **Best Available Fare** | Entitled class mein **cheapest fare**, preferably non-stop, **3-hour slot** mein (00–03, 03–06 … 21–24). Convenience ke liye **10% price band** optimize allowed. [S1] [S2 Q51] [S3 enclosure] |
| **21 days** | “Employees are **encouraged**” — **mandatory nahi**. [S2 Q45] [S3 enclosure] |
| **Proof of cheapest fare** | 20.10.2023 OM ke baad ATA portal par booking **khud hi proof** hai; ATAs sirf cheapest aur 10% band wali flights dikhayenge. Purana screenshot stress ab relaxed. [S3 enclosure] |
| **Ticket category** | Ticket par **“LTC”** likha ho, **“Corporate”** nahi. [S2 Q47] [S3 enclosure] |
| **One ticket per leg** | Ek leg par ek ticket; do ticket hold kar ke baad mein ek cancel karna allowed nahi. [S3 enclosure] |
| **Cancellation < 24 hours** | Self-declared justification; unnecessary cancellation avoid karo. [S3 enclosure] |
| **Cancellation charges** | Airline policy ke mutabik employee bear karta hai; ATA portal charges nil hone chahiye. Reimbursement sirf **official exigency / circumstances beyond control** par. [S1 note 9; S2 Q49, Q54; S3 enclosure] |
| **Mileage points** | Government account travel par **no mileage points**. [S3 enclosure] |
| **Official email na ho** | Admin office employee code, private email, mobile bhej kar ATA registration karwa sakta hai. [S2 Q46] [S3 enclosure] |
| **Unauthorised portal** | Relaxation: Ministry/Department ka **Financial Advisor**, ya subordinate offices mein **HoD not below Joint Secretary**. [S2 Q72] [S3 enclosure] |

> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026) — ATA mandate ka scope:** 2025 Guidelines Note 7(i) kehti hai ki **non-entitled employee jo Special Dispensation ke bahar air travel kare**, uske liye teen ATAs se booking **ab mandatory nahi**, kyunki reimbursement anyway actual air fare ya entitled train/bus fare, **jo kam ho**, tak restricted hai. Lekin **Special Dispensation** mein non-entitled employee ko ATA se hi book karna hoga. [S1 Note 7(ii)] [S3 para 2(ix), enclosure]  
> ⚠️ **Verify with DDO:** 09.09.2026 OM ke saath enclosed 29.08.2022 OM ka purana clause abhi bhi milta hai jo “all non-entitled air travel through three ATAs” bolta hai. Later 10.08.2023 modification aur 2025 consolidated Guidelines ko follow karo; Special Dispensation ka ATA rule dono jagah safe hai. [S1] [S3]

### Non-entitled employee air se jaye, Special Dispensation ke bahar

- Air travel allowed hai, **any airline**.
- Reimbursement = **actual air fare ya entitled train/bus fare for shortest route, whichever is less**. [S1 Note 7(i)]
- 2025 Guidelines ke mutabik is case mein **Best Available Fare tracking / 3-hour slot ka peechha** bhi utna strict nahi, kyunki milna entitled rail/bus fare hi hai. [Lecture min 28; S1]

### Tour packages

- General travel-agent packages **allowed nahi**.
- **ITDC / State Tourism Development Corporations / IRCTC** tours qualify; IRCTC packages mein air + rail + road fare bhi consider ho sakti hai. [S1] [S2 Q36]
- **SPORTS** ke Lakshadweep ship packages bhi allowed, subject to conditions. [S1] [S2 Q44]
- Sirf **transportation / fare component** reimburse; accommodation, food, sightseeing nahi. Operator ko fare component alag show karna aur actual journey ka certificate dena hota hai. [S1] [S2 Q36, Q44]

## Min 16–29 — Special Dispensation: NER / UT J&K / UT Ladakh / A&N

> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** validity **26 September 2026 to 25 September 2028**. [S3] [S4]  
> ❌ **Removed as obsolete:** 2025 Guidelines Note 9 aur 2025 FAQ Q55/Q56 ka **25.09.2026** wala end date; 17.09.2024 wala 2024–26 extension ab historical hai. [S1] [S2] [S8]

### Main conditions

| Point | Rule |
|---|---|
| Conversion | 4-year block mein **ek Home Town LTC** convert kar ke in char regions mein kisi ek jagah. [S3 para 2(i)] |
| HT = HQ | Conversion **nahi**, kyunki HT LTC hi nahi. [S3 para 2(ii)] |
| HT already in covered region | Apne region se hat kar **baaki teen** regions mein se kisi ek. [S3 para 2(iii)] |
| Fresh recruits | 3 HT occasions mein se **ek** conversion + **ek additional** conversion sirf **UT J&K / UT Ladakh**. [S3 para 2(iv)] |
| Air-entitled employee | HQ se apni **entitled class** mein **any airline**, 2022/2023 booking conditions ke saath. [S3 para 2(v)] |
| Non-entitled employee | **Economy class**, any airline, but only specified gateway sectors. [S3 para 2(vi)] |
| All India LTC vs HT conversion | Air facility dono tarah se available; conversion na ho to bhi in regions jaane par air rule apply. [S2 Q60, Q62; S3 para 2(vii)] |
| Annual-HT option wala unmarried employee | 4-year block ki ek occasion convert kar sakta hai. [Lecture min 29; S3] |
| Sikkim | **NER mein included**; Bagdogra (West Bengal) tak air allowed with conditions. [S2 Q34, Q57] |
| Lakshadweep | **Is scheme mein included nahi**. [S2 Q58] |

### Gateway sectors for non-entitled Economy air travel

| Destination | Allowed air sectors |
|---|---|
| NER | **Kolkata / Guwahati ↔ any place in NER** |
| A&N / Port Blair | **Kolkata / Chennai / Visakhapatnam ↔ Port Blair** |
| UT J&K / UT Ladakh | **Delhi / Amritsar ↔ any place in J&K / Ladakh** |

HQ se gateway tak journey **apni normal entitlement** mein. [S3 para 2(vi)]

### Direct HQ-to-region flight

Allowed, even for non-entitled employees, **Economy class, any airline**. Lekin reimbursement ka formula:

> **actual direct air fare** ya **Special Dispensation ke hisaab se entitled fare** — **jo kam ho**, wahi milega. [S3 para 2(viii), enclosure “Provisions for Reimbursements”; S2 Q60]

Iske liye employee ko ATA webpage ka print-out rakhna hota hai: relevant railhead (Kolkata / Guwahati / Delhi / Amritsar / Chennai / Visakhapatnam) se destination tak ka fare, **usi 3-hour slot** mein jis mein direct flight book hui; agar us slot mein flight na ho to **next slot** ka print-out. [S3 enclosure; 2023 OM]

> ✅ **Updated:** **no helicopter**, **no break journey by air**; break journey ka matlab destination ke alawa kisi aur jagah rukna, except connecting flight / layover. [S2 Q50, Q52]

## Min 29–36 — LTC advance: percentage, drawal window, ticket proof

Ye wahi jagah hai jahan lecture sabse zyada purani ho gayi hai.

> ❌ **Removed as obsolete — 90% advance:** lecture min 29 mein “90% advance” bolti hai. Wo 1977-era Government Decision / purane LTC Bill form se aaya tha aur aaj bhi kisi purane DoPT web page par dikhta hai. **2025 consolidated Guidelines ka Rule 15 abhi bhi four-fifths, yani 80%** kehta hai. 2026 tak koi later OM mila nahi jisne 90% ko current banaya ho. [S1]  
> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** max advance = **80% of estimated amount Government would reimburse for both-way journey**. [S1 Rule 15(a)]  
> ⚠️ **Verify with DDO:** agar office ka purana form abhi bhi “90%” print karta ho, to S1 ke Rule 15 aur apne department ke latest sanction order ke hisaab se 80% confirm kar lo.

### Baaki advance rules — zyada tar same hain

| Rule | Position |
|---|---|
| Family alag travel kare | Self aur family ke liye **separate advance** draw ho sakta hai. [S1 Rule 15(b)] |
| Dono outward + return ka advance | Tab, jab leave / anticipated family absence **≤ 3 months / 90 days** ho. [S1 Rule 15(c)] |
| 90 days se zyada | Sirf **outward journey** ka advance. [S1 Rule 15(c)] |
| Pehle dono ka advance le liya, baad mein 90 days cross | **Aadha advance turant refund**. [S1 Rule 15(d)] |
| Outward journey 30 din mein start na ho | **Poora advance refund in full**. [S1 Rule 15(e)] |
| Ticket proof | Advance milne ke **10 din** ke andar outward journey ticket / PNR proof dena **mandatory**. [S1 note 2; S6] |
| Drawal window | **125 din pehle** train journey ke liye; **65 din pehle** air / sea / road ke liye. Ye **obsolete nahi** — 13.05.2016 OM aur 2025 Guidelines dono is OM ko cite karte hain. [S6] [S1] |
| Air-entitled employee ka advance | 2022 OM: ATA webpage ka fare print-out ke saath **at least 30 days before** journey apply karna “may apply” — encouraged, hard mandatory nahi. [S3 enclosure; lecture min 32–33] |
| Special Dispensation advance | HQ → gateway ka rail/bus fare **+** gateway → destination ka ATA-printout air fare. [S3 enclosure] |
| Ordinary non-SD air advance by non-entitled employee | Sirf **rail/bus fare** ke reference se. [S3 enclosure] |

## Min 36–54 — Claim, reimbursement, shortest route, COVID note

### Claim timelines

| Situation | Normal deadline | FA-concurrence relaxation |
|---|---|---|
| **Advance liya** | Return journey complete hone ke **1 month** ke andar claim; warna poora advance ek lump sum mein refund, instalment nahi. [S1 Rule 15(f)] | Claim **3 months** tak, agar **poora advance + penal interest** ek saath refund kiya jaye. [S1 note 1; S2 Q35] |
| **Advance nahi liya** | **3 months**; deri se claim **forfeit**, normal relaxation nahi. [S1 Rule 14] | Ministry/Department + Financial Advisor **6 months** tak admit kar sakte hain, DoPT reference ke bina. [S1 Rule 14 note; S2 Q35, Q71] |

Lecture ka min 37–40 wala example — ₹2 lakh advance, 1 month mein claim na karne par full refund + interest from date of drawal; aur agar ₹18,000 utilise kar ke baaki time par settle kiya to interest sirf **unutilised amount** par — ye logic current timelines ke saath padhna hai. [S1] [S2]

### Reimbursement ka core

- **Point-to-point, through ticket, shortest direct route**; longer route par proportional / entitled-class calculation. [S1 Rule 13; lecture min 44–46]
- **No DA, no incidental expenses, no local journeys** — ghar se airport/station ka taxi/auto bhi nahi. [S1] [S2 Q37, Q38]
- Entitled class ya actual, **jo kam ho**; higher class travel par entitled class tak cap; lower class par actual. [S2 Q53]
- **Reservation charges**, internet/e-ticketing charges (IRCTC website), Government taxes/cess/levies reimburse. Recognised Traveller’s Service Agent ka service charge nahi. [S1 Rule 13 notes]
- **Catering charges** Rajdhani/Shatabdi/Duronto mein fare ke andar compulsory include ho to full fare as per entitlement reimburse. 12.05.2016 OM ne yahi clarify kiya. [S6; lecture min 47]
- **PNR mandatory** rail claims par. [S1 Rule 16 note 2]
- **Children:** under 5 — separate rail berth ka reimbursement nahi, aur non-air-entitled employee ke under-5 child ka air fare nahi, except allowed Special Dispensation segments; **5 to under 12** — actual rail fare, half ya full ticket jo liya ho. [S1] [S2 Q10, Q11]
- **Circular tour ticket:** allowed, par reimbursement HQ → declared place of visit, shortest direct route, entitled class / actual, jo kam ho. [S2 Q39, Q40]

> ❌ **Removed as obsolete — COVID cancellation note:** lecture min 48 mein pandemic-time waiver hai: ticket cancel, advance utilise na ho, penal interest maaf. Ye us period ka special order tha; 2025/2026 consolidated instructions mein nahi hai. Ab cancellation airline/ATA policy, self-declaration, aur official-exigency reimbursement ke hisaab se dekho. [S1] [S3]  
> ✅ **Retained:** 29.03.2023 wala **private vehicle / nil claim + EL encashment** wala clarification. Agar public transport connectivity hai aur employee apni gaadi se gaya, to fare claim `Nil` ho sakta hai, lekin EL encashment milega — bas **journey se pehle LTC avail karne ki intention + leave sanction + EL encashment request + self-declaration** honi chahiye ki declared place actually gaya aur poore journey ka fare nahi maang raha. [S1 Rule 15 note 5(vi); S2 Q22; lecture min 49–50]

| Step | Time |

| Leave sanction | 5 working days + 3 transit days |
| LTC advance / EL encashment sanction | 5 + 3 |

## Min 54–59 — Escort aur misuse / fraudulent claim

**Escort for single differently-abled employee:** LTC par escort allow ho sakti hai, subject to conditions. Lecture min 54–55 ke points:

- employee physically handicapped / single;
- family mein koi **adult member** na ho jo saath jaa sake;
- agar adult family member already hai aur LTC-eligible hai, to alag escort ka case weak ho jata hai;
- medical / competent authority certificate aur conditions lagti hain. [S1 Rule 7 note 16; lecture]

**Rule 16 — fraudulent claim:**

| Stage | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Disciplinary Authority ne fraudulent LTC claim par proceedings decide kiye | Proceedings final hone tak **LTC allow nahi**. [S1] |
| Proceedings mein CCS (CCA) Rule 11 penalty lagi | Pending mein already withheld sets ke alawa **next 2 sets** bhi disallow; written reasons se **2 se zyada** bhi disallow ho sakte hain. [S1; lecture min 58–59] |
| Fully exonerated | Jo sets withheld the, wo **additional sets** ke roop mein future block years mein, **superannuation ki normal date se pehle** mil sakte hain. [S1] |
| Misuse generally | 2026 OM bhi random airline verification aur appropriate action bolta hai. [S3 para 3] |

> ✅ **Updated:** 2026 extension OM mein explicitly likha hai ki Ministries/Departments kuch air tickets **randomly airline se verify** karayein, actual cost vs ticket par dikha cost match ho ya nahi. [S3]

## Min 59–69 — EL encashment with LTC

Ye lecture ka closing hai aur 2025/2024 official position se kaafi match karta hai. [S1 Rule 15 note 5; S7 Rule 38-A; S2 Q21–Q24]

| Rule | Current position |
|---|---|
| Apply kab | **Journey commence hone se pehle**; outward journey se pehle bhi chalega. [S1 note 5(iii); S2 Q23] |
| Ek baar kitna | Max **10 days EL**. [S7 Rule 38-A(1)] |
| Career ceiling | **60 days** total. [S7] |
| Balance | Encashment + leave ke baad **kam se kam 30 EL** credit mein bache. [S7] |
| Cash formula | `(Pay + DA on LTC date) / 30 × number of EL days`; **HRA include nahi**. [S7] |
| Retirement encashment | LTC wali encashment retirement wali 300-day ceiling se **deducted nahi** hoti. [S1 note 5(ii); S7] |
| Dono husband-wife Central Govt servants | Dono apna **10 days each** le sakte hain, apne-apne 60-day career cap ke saath. [S1] [S2 Q24] |
| Family alag group / alag time | Ek hi LTC ke liye **ek hi encashment**, chahe employee aur family alag travel karein. [S1 note 5(i); S2 Q21] |
| Employee khud na jaye, family jaye | Employee family member ki journey se pehle apply kar ke EL encashment le sakta hai. [S2 Q21] |
| Private vehicle / `Nil` claim | Upar wale 2023 conditions ke saath EL encashment allowed. [S1] [S2 Q22] |
| Spouse PSU / autonomous body / Railway facility | Central Govt employee spouse ki facility use kare ya spouse ke Railway pass se travel kare, to bhi EL encashment allow, subject to conditions. [S1 note 5(iv), (vii)] |
| Re-employed pensioner | EL encashment possible, subject to general 10/60/30 conditions. [Lecture min 67; S1] |
| LTC avail na ho | Poora encashed amount **interest ke saath refund**, aur **EL credit-back**. Interest = **2% above GPF/PF rate**, drawal se recovery tak. [S7 Rule 38-A(5); lecture min 68–69] |

Lecture ka min 63–64 wala 300 vs 250 EL retirement example isliye important hai: LTC encashment retirement wali maximum se alag rakhi gayi hai, isliye “60 EL LTC mein encash kar li to retirement par 300 ki jagah 240 hi milega” wala purana dar galat hai. [S1] [S7]

## Min 65–69 — Rule 17, 18, 19 aur closing

Lecture Swami compilation ka reference dekar khatam hoti hai. Official skeleton:

- **Rule 17 — Interpretation:** kisi rule par doubt ho to matter **DoPT** decide karega. [S1]
- **Rule 18 — Power to relax:** Ministry/Department **undue hardship** mein written reasons ke saath rule relax kar sakta hai, lekin **DoPT ki concurrence** ke bina nahi. [S1] [S2 Q73]
- **Rule 19 — Saving:** jo purani instructions in rules se contradict nahi karti, ya jin points par rule kuch bolta hi nahi, wo tab tak chalti hain jab tak amend / modify / cancel na ho. [S1]

> ⚠️ **Verify with DDO:** Rule 19 ka matlab ye nahi ki 1977 ka 90% form ya 2022 ka blanket ATA clause automatically zinda hai. 2023/2025/2026 ke later instruments ne un points ko modify kar diya hai; isi liye unhe ❌ **Removed as obsolete** tag kiya gaya hai.
