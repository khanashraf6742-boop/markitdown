## Min 00–03 — Rule 8: LTC ke types

Lecture Part I ke pehle do points recap ke baad **Types of LTC** par aati hai.

| Type | Frequency | Point yaad rakhne wali |
|---|---|---|
| **Home Town (HT) LTC** | Once in a block of **2 calendar years** | Distance kitni bhi ho — 0 km ho ya 1000 km — HT LTC admissible hai, **agar HT aur HQ alag hon**. [S1] [S2] |
| **All India / Any Place in India LTC** | Once in a **4-year block** | Ye **ek HT LTC ke badle** adjust hoti hai; isliye HT wale employee ke paas 4 saal mein 2 sets: ek HT, ek All India — ya dono HT, depending on option. [S1] [S2] |
| **Family-away annual HT option** | **Har saal**, self only | Agar family HT / HQ se alag rehti hai, to employee baaki saari concessions chhod kar **sirf apne liye har saal HT LTC** le sakta hai. [S1 Rule 8(c); lecture] |

**HT = HQ case:** Headquarters aur declared Home Town same ho to **Home Town LTC nahi**. Aise employee ko 4-year block mein **sirf ek Any Place in India LTC** milegi; HT conversion ka sawaal hi nahi. [S2 Q14, Q19, Q59]

> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** 2025 FAQ Q17 kehti hai ki HT = HQ wale employee **2026 jaise pehle saal mein 2 All India LTC** le sakte hain — ek 2022–25 ka unavailed carry-forward, ek 2026–29 ka naya block. Ye “two LTCs in one calendar year” ka exception hai, do naye sets nahi. [S2]

## Min 03–11 — Fresh recruits: 8 calendar-year occasions

Ye lecture ka sabse zyada example-wala hissa hai. Rule aaya **DoPT OM dated 23.09.2008**, 6th CPC ke baad. [S1 Rule 7 note 13; S2 Q64–Q70]

### Core rule

- **Pehle 8 saal**, **initial joining date se personal** blocks; general 2018–21 / 2022–25 / 2026–29 se match nahi karte. [S2 Q65, Q66]
- Eligibility **1 year regular service complete** hone ke baad, aur **calendar-year wise** count hoti hai.
- Pattern: **1st, 2nd, 3rd = HT**; **4th = All India**; **5th, 6th, 7th = HT**; **8th = All India**. [S2 Q64]
- **Carry-forward nahi:** jo saal ka LTC nahi liya, wo 31 December ko lapse. [S2 Q67]
- **HT = HQ fresh recruit:** 1st/2nd/3rd, 5th/6th/7th HT occasions nahi milenge; sirf **4th aur 8th All India LTC** milegi. [S2 Q70]
- Fresh recruit **normal LTC rules opt** kar sakta hai, lekin aisa karne par us block mein fresh-recruit wali extra LTCs nahi milegi. [S2 Q66]
- 23.09.2008 se pehle join kar chuke aur us din tak 8 saal complete na kiye ho, to bache hue saal ke hisaab se every-year facility milti hai / option diya gaya. [Lecture min 11–12; S1 note 13]

### Lecture ka 22.05.2023 example

| Stage | Result |
|---|---|
| Joining | **22.05.2023** |
| 1 year complete | **22.05.2024** |
| 1st LTC | **HT**, 22.05.2024 se 31.12.2024 ke beech journey start |
| 2nd LTC | **HT**, calendar 2025 |
| 3rd LTC | **HT**, calendar 2026 |
| 4th LTC | **All India**, calendar 2027 |
| 5th–7th | HT, 2028–2030 |
| 8th | All India, 2031 |

> ⚠️ **Verify with DDO:** ye illustration lecture ka hai; 2022–25 wale general block se isko compare mat karo. Fresh recruit ka apna personal calendar clock hai.

### Official 2025 FAQ ke current illustrations

| Joining date | Eligibility starts | 1st | 4th | 8th | Uske baad |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **01.09.2016** | 01.09.2017 | HT, 01.09.2017–31.12.2017 | All India, 2020 | All India, 2024 | **2025 Nil**, phir **2026–29 normal entitlement** [S2 Table-3] |
| **31.12.2015** | 31.12.2016 | HT, **sirf 31.12.2016** — journey usi din commence ho | All India, 2019 | All India, 2023 | 2024–25 HT, phir **2026–29 normal** [S2 Table-4] |
| **01.01.2025** | 01.01.2026 | HT, 2026 | All India, 2029 | All India, 2033 | **2034–37 regular block** se normal [S2 Table-5] |

**31 December wali trick:** joining 31.12.2015 ho to 1 saal 31.12.2016 ko poora hota hai, aur calendar year bhi usi din khatam. Isliye pehli HT LTC ka **outward journey usi ek din start** hona chahiye; return 2017 mein ho sakti hai. [S2 Q69; lecture min 16]

## Min 11–19 — 8 saal ke baad running block mein kaise aayenge

Lecture purane running blocks — 2006–09, 2010–13, 2014–17, 2018–21, 2022–25 — board par likhkar samjhati hai. Logic aaj bhi wahi hai, bas **ab running block 2026–29** hai.

**Simple rule:** 8th / aakhri fresh-recruit LTC jis regular 4-year block mein girti hai, usi ke hisaab se aage normal entitlement decide hoti hai. 2025 FAQ Q68 isko teen cases mein batati hai: [S2]

1. 8th LTC regular block ke **second sub-block ke pehle saal** mein aaye → us block mein sirf **HT** bachega, kyunki 8th par All India mil chuka tha.
2. 8th LTC **second sub-block ke doosre saal** mein aaye → us saal **koi LTC nahi**.
3. 8th LTC **naye regular block ke shuru** mein aaye → seedha **normal LTC rules**.

Lecture ke purane examples:

- 8th LTC 2016 ya 2017 mein → agla LTC **2018–21 block** se. [Lecture min 20–22]
- 8th LTC 2019 mein, yani 2018–21 ke pehle do saal mein → 2021 mein ek LTC milti hai, phir 2022–25 normal. [Lecture min 24–26]
- 10.05.2006 join karne wala 23.09.2008 OM ke beech mein aa gaya → 2007 wali pehli LTC count, 2008 ko 2nd occasion maan kar aage 3 HT + 4th All India pattern chalaya. [Lecture min 27–30]

> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** in purane saalon ko **date examples** ki tarah padho, current answer ki tarah nahi. Aaj same logic ko **2026–29 / 2030–33** par apply karo. [S2 Q66, Q68, Table-5]

## Min 19–31 — Special Dispensation: HT LTC ko convert karna

Lecture yahan “teen jagah” bolti hai: **North East, Jammu & Kashmir, Andaman & Nicobar**. Official 2026 OM aur 2025 FAQ **char** regions batate hain.

> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** covered regions = **NER, UT of Jammu & Kashmir, UT of Ladakh, A&N Islands**. **Sikkim NER mein included** hai; **Lakshadweep is scheme mein nahi**. [S2 Q57, Q58] [S3]  
> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** scheme **26.09.2026 se 25.09.2028** tak valid hai. 2024 OM wali 26.09.2024–25.09.2026 validity aur 2025 FAQ Q55/Q56 ka 25.09.2026 wala answer **superseded** ho chuka hai. [S3] [S4]

### Conversion ka basic set

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Normal eligible employee | 4-year block mein **ek Home Town LTC** convert kar ke in char regions mein se kisi ek jagah ja sakta hai. [S3 para 2(i)] |
| HT = HQ | HT LTC hai hi nahi, to conversion **nahi**. [S2 Q59] [S3 para 2(ii)] |
| Home Town khud in char regions mein ho | Apne region ke andar normal HT jaa sakte ho; **conversion** sirf **baaki teen regions** mein se kisi ek ke liye. [S2 Q61] [S3 para 2(iii)] |
| Pehle hi ek HT LTC avail kar liya | Ab All India LTC se jaa sakte ho, lekin usse **Special Dispensation conversion** nahi kahenge; air facility par alag conditions. [S2 Q62] [S3 para 2(vii)] |
| 4-year block mein do visits | Ek HT conversion + ek All India LTC, dono possible, subject to scheme validity aur conditions. [S2 Q63] |
| Fresh recruit | 3 HT occasions mein se **ek** convert; plus **ek additional HT conversion** sirf **UT J&K / UT Ladakh** ke liye. [S3 para 2(iv)] |
| Unmarried employee jisne “har saal HT, self only” option liya | 4-year block ki **ek occasion** ko in regions ke liye convert kar sakta hai. [Lecture min 31–32; S3 paras 2(i), 2(iv)] |

> ❌ **Removed as obsolete:** lecture ka “J&K / NER / A&N — bas teen regions”, “25.09.2026 tak hi hai”, aur 2024 OM ka “26.09.2024 se 25.09.2026” wala line. [S3] [S8]

## Min 31–34 — Carry-forward aur Rule 11

### Regular block vs fresh recruit

| Item | Regular 2-year / 4-year block | Fresh recruit every-year LTC |
|---|---|---|
| Grace | Block / sub-block **automatically 1 saal** extend, **31 December of next calendar year** tak. [S2 Q18] | **No carry-forward**; saal khatam = LTC lapse. [S2 Q67] |
| 2026 mein practical effect | 2024–25 ka unavailed LTC **2026** mein, aur 2026–27 ka naya LTC bhi **2026** mein. [S2 Q16] | 2026 ka personal occasion 2026 mein hi avail karna hoga. |
| Claim timeline | Return journey ke baad 3 months (no advance) / 1 month (advance). [S1] [S2 Q35] | Same claim rules. |

### Rule 11 — same place ya alag place

- Employee aur family **alag groups** mein, **alag time** par, **alag destinations** bhi jaa sakte hain. [S1 Rule 11; S2 Q6]
- Kuch family members **Home Town** aur kuch **Anywhere in India** bhi same 2-year block mein avail kar sakte hain. [S2 Q7]
- LTC **us block mein count** hoti hai jisme **outward journey commence** hui, chahe return journey block khatam hone ke baad ho. [S1 Rule 9]

> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** 2025 FAQ Q8 ke mutabik spouse / children ka HQ se alag rehna allowed hai; unke travel ko Rule 11 ke under alag group / alag destination ke saath dekha jata hai. [S2]

| Topic | One-line answer |
