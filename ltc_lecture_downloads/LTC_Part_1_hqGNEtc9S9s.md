## Min 00–05 — Intro, Rule 1, aur “kaun covered hai”

Lecture shuru hota hai `Hello friends` se. Speaker clear karti hai: ye **Just Crack It** ki LTC series hai, **Swamy Handbook Section 13** se, aur target hai **SO LDCE / UPSC-type exam**. Isliye approach concept + MCQ point-of-view hai: “rule yaad karo, uska reason samjho, phir exception mark karo.”

**Rule 1 — short title aur commencement.** In rules ka naam **CCS (Leave Travel Concession) Rules, 1988** hai. **21 May 1988** se force mein aaye. Exam mein ye date directly poochhi ja sakti hai.

**Rule 1(3) — jin par apply hota hai:**

1. Central civil services / posts ke Government servants, including **civilian** employees in Defence Services. Civilian word important hai: Armed Forces member nahi.
2. State Government employee jo **Central Government par deputation** par hai.
3. **Contract** par appoint kiya gaya Government servant.
4. Retirement ke baad **re-employed** person.

Speaker ka concept simple hai: in charo mein service ka **fixed / anticipated period** hota hai. LTC block-based concession hai — 2 saal ya 4 saal — isliye jis appointment ka koi pata hi nahi ki kab khatam hoga, usse is scheme mein nahi la sakte.

**Rule 1(4) — jin par apply nahi hota:**

- non–whole-time employment;
- casual / daily-rated / contingency-paid staff;
- Railway servants;
- Armed Forces members;
- Indian Missions abroad ke local recruits;
- aur koi bhi person jise **koi aur travel concession** already mil rahi ho.

> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** 2025 consolidated Guidelines bhi yahi list deti hain, with one important note: **Railway employees ko, conditions ke saath, “All India LTC” once in a 4-year block allow kiya ja sakta hai** — matlab blanket exclusion ka matlab “kabhi kuch nahi” nahi, balki normal CCS (LTC) Rules se bahar rakh kar special option dena hai. [S1]

## Min 05–10 — Rule 2: special categories ke liye extra conditions

Regular, permanent Central Government servant ke liye seedha rule: **1 year continuous service** on the date of journey (self ya family, jisne bhi travel kiya ho). [S1] [S2]

Lekin Rule 1(3) ki **deputation / contract / re-employment** wali teen categories ke liye Rule 2 extra certificate maangta hai:

| Category | Minimum service | Extra certification |
|---|---|---|
| State Govt → Central deputation | 1 year continuous Central service | Likely to serve **at least 2 years** for Home Town LTC, **at least 4 years** for All India LTC, counted from Central joining date |
| Contract appointment | 1 year continuous service | Same 2 / 4-year anticipation; agar 1-year contract baad mein extend hua to **total contract duration** count hogi |
| Re-employment after retirement | 1 year continuous service | Same, plus continuity rule |

**Contract wali baat:** agar pehle 1 saal ka contract tha aur baad mein extend hua, to sirf pehle 1 saal ko nahi, **poori total duration** ko dekha jayega. Lecture isse “fixed period” wale concept se connect karti hai.

**Re-employment wali baat:** retirement aur re-employment ke beech **koi break nahi** ho, to purani service + re-employed service ko continuous mana jayega. Lekin iska matlab double benefit nahi. Example lecture mein: kisi ne retirement se pehle hi us 4-year block ka **All India LTC** avail kar liya, aur 11.10.2025 se re-employed hua — to usi block ka All India LTC dobara nahi milega. [S1]

> ⚠️ **Verify with DDO:** 2025 Guidelines ka Rule 7 note ye bhi kehta hai ki **technical resignation** ke baad doosre Government post mein join karne par LTC entitlement carry forward ho sakti hai; aur 8 saal ke andar aisa karne wale ko **initial appointment date se hi fresh recruit** mana jayega. Ye lecture ka main track nahi hai, but current clarification hai. [S1]

## Min 10–15 — Railway privilege pass aur “All India LTC only”

Lecture ka ye hissa exam mein kaafi productive hai.

Railway employees ke paas apna **privilege pass / free travel facility** hoti hai. Isliye normally CCS (LTC) Rules unpar apply nahi hote. Lekin ek conditional route hai:

- passes **surrender** karne ka certificate dena hota hai;
- milta hai **All India LTC**, **Home Town LTC nahi**;
- isliye HT LTC ko NER / J&K / Ladakh / A&N mein convert karne wali Special Dispensation bhi in par available nahi hoti;
- surrender jis calendar year ka hai, **journey usi year mein start** honi chahiye; sirf return agle saal ho to bhi chalega;
- block ka last year automatic **one-year grace** tak extend hota hai, isliye surrender last year ka ho to us grace year tak journey start kar sakte hain. [S1] [S2]

Lecture ye bhi kehti hai: jo Central servant Railways mein deputation par hai aur wahan privilege pass ka entitlement hai, usi “already concession available” wale logic se normal LTC nahi milega.

> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** 2025 FAQ confirms: **HT = HQ wale employee ko HT LTC nahi**, aur isliye Special Dispensation ke liye HT conversion ka sawaal hi nahi uthta. [S2] Railway surrender wali baat ko is broader rule ke saath padho.

## Min 15–31 — Rule 4: “Family” kaun hai, kaun nahi

Ye Part I ka sabse lamba aur sabse important hissa hai. Speaker board par ek family tree banati hai: spouse, 2 sons + 2 daughters, parents, step-parents, brothers/sisters, divorced/abandoned daughters, grandchildren… phir ek-ek karke cut karti hai.

### Family mein included

| Relation | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Spouse** (husband / wife) | Always eligible; **dependency test nahi**; residence condition nahi. Husband-wife “one unit” maane jate hain. [S1] [S2] |
| **Two eldest surviving unmarried children**, including step-children aur legally adopted children | **Wholly dependent**; alag reh sakte hain, phir bhi eligible. Age limit nahi likhi — unmarried + wholly dependent hona chahiye. [S1] [S2] |
| **Divorced / abandoned / separated / widowed daughters** | **Wholly dependent + residing with** Government servant. Unke **children** family mein nahi aate. [S1] [S2] |
| **Parents / step-parents** | **Wholly dependent**; saath rehna zaroori nahi. [S1] [S2] |
| **Unmarried minor brothers**; unmarried / divorced / abandoned / separated / widowed **sisters** | **Residing with + wholly dependent**, aur parents either not alive ya khud Government servant par wholly dependent. Sisters ke **children** bhi excluded. [S1] [S2] |

### Family se bahar

- **Parents-in-law / sas-sasur, saali, jeth etc.** — nahi. [S2 Q5]
- **Grandparents, grandchildren** — nahi. [S2]
- Divorced/abandoned/separated/widowed daughter ya sister ke **bachche** — nahi. [S1] [S2]
- **Not more than one wife**, except jahan doosri legally wedded wife ke liye **Government ki specific permission** ho. [S1]
- Spouse ka private-sector employer se LTA / travel reimbursement lena allowed hai; is par restriction nahi. [S2 Q9]

### Dependency ka matlab

> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** 2025 FAQ ke mutabik jis family member ki **income from all sources, including pension + temporary increase in pension, minimum pension (presently Rs 9,000 per month under 7th CPC) + us par Dearness Relief (DR)** se zyada na ho, wahi “wholly dependent” mana jayega. Spouse par ye condition apply hi nahi hoti. [S2 Q2]  
> Lecture mein “Rs 9,000 + DA/DR” sunayi deta hai; official word **DR** aur **minimum family pension** se juda hai — answer mein wahi likho.

### Bachchon ki 2-child restriction — 20.10.1997

Lecture isse **Fifth CPC / small family norm** se connect karti hai. Restriction **20 October 1997** se aayi. Exceptions:

1. jinke **20.10.1997 se pehle hi 2 se zyada** bachche the;
2. restriction ke **1 saal ke andar** paida hue bachche;
3. **second child birth mein multiple birth** (judwa/triplets) ki wajah se 2 se upar count. [S1] [S2]

> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** 2025 FAQ explicitly kehti hai: **25 saal se upar ka unmarried son/daughter bhi eligible ho sakta hai**, bas **unmarried + wholly dependent** ho. Isliye “25 = automatic cut-off” wali purani exam shorthand mat use karo. [S2 Q4]

### Residence / family-away examples

Speaker ek diagram use karti hai: HQ city A, hometown city B, maa city B mein, bachcha padhai ke liye city C mein.

- Family **HQ par saath** reh rahi hai aur HT gayi → **Home Town LTC** count.
- Family HQ par hai aur kisi aur place / even HT gayi in a different pattern → All India LTC count, depending on option. [S2 Q74]
- Family **HQ se alag** reh rahi hai aur HT gayi → HT LTC count, lekin fare **HQ–HT distance ya actual, jo kam ho** tak restricted. [S2 Q75]
- Family alag jagah se **HQ / kisi aur place** gayi aur HT involve nahi → **All India LTC** count. [S2 Q75]

> ✅ **Updated (10 Nov 2026):** spouse aur dependent children ka HQ se alag rehna allowed hai; 2025 FAQ Q8 isko directly confirm karti hai. [S2] Suspended employee khud LTC nahi le sakta, lekin **family** le sakti hai. [S1] [S2 Q12]

## Min 31–40 — Rule 5: Home Town declaration aur change

**Initial declaration:** Government servant apna hometown declare karta hai; **Controlling Officer** use accept karta hai. Shuruat mein koi detailed check nahi — 1977 ke simplified procedure se yahi aaya hai. [S1]

**Change:** poori service mein **sirf ek baar**, aur **exceptional circumstances** mein. Jab change maanga jaye tab detailed check hota hai. Authority:

- normal case: **Head of Department (HoD)**;
- agar Government servant khud HoD hai: **Administrative Ministry / Department**. [S1]

**Basic test:** kya ye wo jagah hai jahan employee **normally rehta**, agar Government service ki wajah se HQ par na rehna padta? [S1]

Lecture ke **four criteria ek ke baad ek** apply hote hain — saath mein nahi:

1. Us jagah **extended family** rehti hai aur domestic/social obligations ki wajah se baar-baar physical presence zaroori hoti hai. “Kitni frequency?” define nahi hai.
2. Us jagah employee ke naam **residential property** hai — individual ya joint name, dono chalega.
3. Us jagah **near relatives** hain, aur unka rehna **more or less permanent** nature ka ho; 2–4 mahine ka temporary stay kaafi nahi.
4. Government service join karne se **pehle kuch saal** employee wahan reh chuka ho; “some years” ki koi fixed 4/5 saal definition nahi.

Agar property 3 cities mein ho, to employee ek propose kar sakta hai, lekin **Controlling Officer ka satisfaction** final hai. Reason justified na lage to change reject ho sakta hai — aur ye strict check sirf **change** ke time hai, initial declaration par nahi. [S1]

## Min 40–44 — Rule 6: Place of visit declare karna

All India LTC ke liye **intended place of visit pehle se Controlling Officer ko declare** karna hota hai. [S1]

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Journey **shuru hone se pehle** change | Controlling Officer ki approval / immediate information ke saath change. [Lecture + S1] |
| Journey **shuru hone ke baad** change | Sirf **exceptional circumstances**, reason **beyond control**, aur **HoD / Administrative Ministry** ki relaxation. [S1] |
| Changed place | Preferably usi route par padna chahiye jahan tak employee actually pahuncha ho. |

**Nathula Pass / Gangtok example:** employee ne Nathula Pass declare kiya; Gangtok se aage landslide, permit band, ya weather ki wajah se ja nahi paya. Usne place of visit badal kar **Gangtok** kar diya. Claim milega, kyunki:

- rukavat employee ke control mein nahi thi;
- Gangtok declared destination ke **route par** padta tha;
- isliye “actually declare kiya tha wahan pahuncha” ka evidence issue resolve hua. [Lecture; principle in S1 Rule 6]

> ⚠️ **Verify with DDO:** lecture “before journey = CO approval, after journey = HoD only” bolti hai; 2025 Guidelines ka Rule 6 text “exceptional circumstances… relaxation by Administrative Ministry/Department or HoD” kehta hai. Exam mein S1 ka wording zyada safe hai.

## Min 44–61 — Leave, class, outward/return, transfer/tour, suspension, LPR

### Leave

- LTC **kisi bhi leave** ke dauran avail ho sakta hai: CL, special casual leave, EL, **CCL**, **study leave**. Vacation department mein vacation ko regular leave mana jata hai. [S1] [S2 Q25]
- **Sirf weekend / holidays** par, bina leave liye, LTC nahi. [S1 Note 2; S2 Q27]
- Sirf family travel kar rahi ho to employee ki leave zaroori nahi. [S2 Q26]
- Journey se pehle Controlling Officer ko inform karna aur actual journey ka evidence dena zaroori hai; service record mein bhi LTC avail ki entry hoti hai. [S1]

### Class at journey commencement

Jo **entitlement journey start ke time** thi, wahi count hogi — baad mein promotion/change se class automatically nahi badalti. [S1 Rule 7 Note 1(ii)]

### Outward-only / return-only reimbursement

Lecture ke examples:

- sirf outward journey ka claim kabhi-kabhi allow hota hai; sirf return journey ka bhi — jaise employee HT gaya, wahan shaadi ho gayi, aur spouse ke saath return kiya to spouse ka return fare allow. [S1 note 6; lecture]
- **Under 5 child:** railway mein ticket nahi lagti; separate berth ka reimbursement nahi. **5 se under 12:** employee ne half ticket li ya full berth, jo bhi actual rail fare diya, wahi reimburse. [S1] [S2 Q10, Q11]
- Non-air-entitled employee ke **under-5 child ka air fare** reimburse nahi, except allowed Special Dispensation segments. [S1] [S2 Q11]

### Transfer / tour / study leave / suspension / LPR

- **Transfer ya tour ke saath** HT / All India LTC combine ho sakti hai; option TA claim ke time exercise karni hoti hai; reimbursement shortest-route / entitled-class comparison se nikalti hai. [S1 Rule 7 notes 8, 12]
- Training deputation par **training place automatically headquarters** ban jata hai; SR 59 ke under competent authority ne HQ badla to LTC ke liye bhi naya HQ count. [S1]
- **Study leave** par bhi LTC admissible hai, lekin reimbursement usi maximum distance / entitlement tak. [S1 Rule 7 note 3 + lecture]
- **Suspension:** suspended employee ko leave nahi milti, isliye khud LTC nahi; **family members** le sakte hain. [S1 Rule 7 note 17; S2 Q12]
- **Leave Preparatory to Retirement (LPR):** HT aur All India LTC dono ways allow, bas **return journey LPR khatam hone se pehle complete** ho. [S1 Rule 4 note 4; lecture]
