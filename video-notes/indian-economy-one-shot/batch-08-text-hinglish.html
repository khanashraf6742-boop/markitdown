1 · agriculture ka mahatvAgriculture & its share

ab hum log kar lete hain apna agriculture end elaid sektars. dekho, jitna bolun na sirf utna hi yaad rakhna hai. ismein saare pivaikyu hain jo bhi exam mein poochhe gaye hain — kuch pivaikyu aj bhi relevant hain, kuch nahi hain. to jo important hai na sirf usi pe focus karenge.

kisan divas kab manaya jata hai? 23 disanbar ko. ye barth enivarsari hai hamare phormar PM chaudhari charan sinh ki. thand ke mahine mein 23 kisan — aise yaad kar low.

ab agriculture ka jo share hai GDP aur national income mein vo dikriz hota ja raha hai. 1950 mein 52% GDP mein share tha, ab ye almost 20% ke asapas rah gaya. to ye achhi baat hai ya buri baat hai? agar economic Perspective se bolun to ye achhi baat hai, kyunki economic thyori kahati hai ki jab agriculture mein population ka parasentej dikriz hota hai, to iska matlab hai ki hamari population kahin dusre productive sector mein kaam kar rahi hai.

Bharat mein 46% population kheti pe dependent hai. agar hum do cheez kar sakte hain — ya to unako altaranetiv de dein, kheti se alag koi altaranetiv, ya phir agriculture mein hi reform kar dein.

agriculture pe jo income tax hai vo State government ke under hai — ye aap log yaad rakhna. aur UP mein jo larjest share of varkars hai vo agriculture sector mein hai, araund 59.3%.

agriculture sector ne Third Five Year Plan mein jo hai negative growth rate rajistar ki thi. negative ka matlab? jab production kam hone lage — 100 tan tha, phir 98 ho gaya, 99 ho gaya. kovid ke time par saare sector mein negative growth thi sivay agriculture ko chhod ke. ab 1961-65 mein aisa kya hua hoga? us time par chal rahe the war — China ke saath war, Pakistan ke saath war — aur usi ke baad jab agriculture mein negative growth ai to 1966 mein grin Resolution, "jay javan jay kisan" ka slogan, India self saphishiensi hona chahiye food grain mein — to vo saari cheezein focus ki gain.

2 · crops, revolutions aur bhoomi reformCrops & land reforms

rais jo hai na vo main food krop hai aur larjest prodyusar iska vest Bengal hai. vit mein UP larjest pe aata hai. shugaraken mein India second larjest pe aata hai, brazil ke baad. kophi ka 70% hamara Karnataka se dekhne ko milta hai. blaik soil kis liye popular hai? kotan ke liye.

commercial yaani kaish krop vo hoti hai jisako is parpas se ugaya jata hai ki market mein jaake bech diya jaayega aur paisa aa jaayega. jabaki food krops normal — jaise gehun, chaval — khud ke liye ugai jati hai, self kanjampashan ke liye. commercial krop mein kya-kya aata hai? kotan, shugaraken, potaito, repasid, jut, tobaiko, rabad, ti aur kophi.

bulak capitalist kya hote hain? ye vo pharmar hote hain jinke pass "possess means but are not rich" — means ye self enployad hai, self phainensd hai. ye vard kisne koin kiya tha? loyad aur susain rudolph ne 1987 mein. concept kya hai? ek kisan hai, grain ugata hai, bullock cart se mandi tak le jata hai, bechakar profit kamata hai aur profit se apni jamin, trade, bail mein inavest karta hai — dhire-dhire smol capitalist banta hai.

demand for agriculture produce jo hai vo inailastik hai. matlab? pehle chaval ₹50 kilo the to family 10 kilo kharidti thi; ab chaval ₹90 kilo ho gaye to family 8 kilo, 9 kilo khareed legi — par kharidegi zaroor. yaani nesesiti wali cheezon ki demand inailastik hoti hai. to kisan agar ovar production karta hai na to praises aur revenyu se bhi usko dikkat hoti hai.

ab jab hum land reform bolte hain to usmein teen-chaar cheezein aati hain. pehla — abolishan of jamindari, yaani jo jamindar intaramidiet middlemen hote the unako eliminet karna. doosra — land holding pe siling lagana ki ek insaan itni hi jamin rakh sakata hai. phir tenensi reform — jo tenent rent pe jamin lete the, unako regulet karna. phir security of tenyor — kitne time ke liye rent pe liya hai. aur ownership on tenent — ki bhaiya tenent ko ownership di jaegi ya nahi.

ab ye kaitegri hai — marjinal pharmar unako bolte hain jinke pass ek hekteyar se kam jamin hoti hai. smol unako bolte hain jinke pass do hekteyar se kam — ek se do ke beech mein. isliye hamare yahan smol end marjinal ek saath bolte hain, aur ye age of now 89% hai, letest Economic Survey ke according. semidiyam — do se chaar hekteyar; midiyam — chaar se 10 hekteyar; large — 10 hekteyar se zyada, aur ye bahut hi negijibal hai. semidiyam se ye wala mostly aapko Punjab-Haryana wale rijan mein dekhne ko milega.

agriculture sector mein low productivity kyun hai? sabse pehla — population preshar on land. disguised unemployment — ek chhota sa kaam hai, us pe chaar jane lage hue hain; teen bandon ko hata bhi doge to bhi koi farak nahi padega. smol land holding — agar chhoti si jamin hai to aap zyada experiment nahi kar sakte, commercial of scale nahi hoga. aur ye saari cheezein rular poverty bhi promote karti hain.

3 · agriculture bord, export aur varchual waterBoards & exports

ab ye agriculture bord — yahan se bhi question aate hain. kophi bord 1942 mein estaiblish kiya gaya, hed kvartar bengaluru mein. rabar bord 1947 mein, kottayam mein hed kvartar. ti bord 1954 mein, kolakata mein. tanbaku bord 1976 mein, guntur mein. spaises bord 1987 mein, kochchi mein. meet end poltri 2009 mein, Delhi mein. grep prosesing 2009 mein, pune mein. phisharij 2006 mein, haidarabad mein. jut bord 2009 mein, kolakata mein. aur ek abhi naya bana hai jiska hed kvartar nizamabad (telangana) mein hai — vo national tarmerik bord hai.

maiksimam phoren arnar amang kophi, basamati, kotan, ti — inamen se poocha jaaye to maiksimam basamati rais hai, kyunki hum basamati rais galph countries mein bhejte hain aur wahan se phoren exchange milta hai. most vailyuebal agriculture export aitam kya hai? rais. prephard export maingo vairayti? alfanso. aur malabari silk mein liding State? Karnataka.

ek kilo chaval ko ugane mein 3000 se 5000 litar paani lagata hai. aur hum isi krop ko export kar dete hain — yaani kahin na kahin hum inadayarektali paani ko bhi bahar bhej rahe hain. isi ko hum log varchual water bolte hain — jo water intensiv krop ka agar hum bahar bhejenge to usi ko varchual water bolenge.

4 · khadya surakshaFood security & NFSA

National Food Security Act kab aaya? 10 September 2013 mein. ye kya cover karta hai? 75% rular population ko, aur 50% arban population ko. National Food Security Act ke andar 5 kilo rais, vit, kors gen — ye sab diye jate hain, par person par manth.

vit hum log dete hain ₹2 kilo, rais ₹3 kilo, kors ₹1 kilo. aur jo eldest vuman hai jiski umr 18 saal se upar hai, vo hed of hausahold kahalai jaegi; rashan card pe usi ka naam hoga. special focus diya gaya ki vuman aur childran ko nutrition saport mile, kyunki hamare yahan hidan hunger ka concept zyada hai.

ab ek question aata hai — which of d following ar d dayamenshan of food security? ismein kya aa jaayega? 3A — avelebiliti, eksesabiliti aur aphordebiliti. avelebal to hona chahiye, rich ke andar hona chahiye (40 kilometre door na jana pade), aur affordable bhi hona chahiye. yutilaijeshan aur stebiliti bhi aa jate hain, par ye pehle teen zaroori hain. yaad rakhna — akauntebiliti koi dayamenshan nahi hai.
