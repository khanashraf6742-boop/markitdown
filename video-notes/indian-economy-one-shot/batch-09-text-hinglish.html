1 · HDI aur khushi ke indexHDI & happiness

ab ye baar-baar puchhegi ki India ki rank kitni hai? to aap logon ko malum hona chahiye ki India ki rank kitni hai ismein. but usse pehle dayamenshan dekh lete hain. sabse pehla hai health — health min life eksapektensi et barth. uske baad hai education — min aur eksapekted Year of skuling. Third hai standard of living. to ye teeno cheezein aap logon ko exam ke liye yaad rakhni hain.

letest jo report avelebal hai — 2025 ki Human Development Report — usmein India ki rainking 130 hai, total 193 country mein se. ye aap logon ko please remember it, kyunki yahan se question baar-baar poocha jata hai, ek baar nahi poocha jata. aur taital bhi yaad rakh lena — “e maitar of chois: people end posibilitiz in d age of eai.”

uske baad gros national haippines indeks, jo bhutan ne adopt kiya tha 1972 mein. inke king the jigme singye vangachuk. ye based hai life setisphekshan, life eksapektensi aur environment par. kahin na kahin riyal indicator inhi ko mana jata hai.

2 · health policy aur mishanHealth policy & missions

ab hum log aage badhte hain aur dekhate hain schemes ko, health programme se related. national health policy kab ai? national health policy ai 1983 mein. rular health mishan aaya 2005 mein. pehle to inhone sirf health policy nikali, phir inhone socha yaar ganv ke liye alag honi chahiye — to almost 22 saal baad nikali.

intigreted child development services 1975 mein ai. kaise yaad karoge? ek saath paanch bachchon ki dekhrekh karna — ek saath paanch. uske baad aapko yaad rakhna hai janani suraksha yojana 2005 mein ai thi. bhaiya jab ek saath aap paanch bachchon ki dekhrekh karoge, yaani government ek saath paanch bachchon ki dekhrekh kar rahi hai, to usko jo paida karne wali man hai uski bhi karegi ya nahi? to janani bhi 2005 mein ho gayi.

phir chhah saal baad dono ka — janani ka bhi, shishu ka bhi — janani shishu suraksha programme 2011 mein. uske baad Mission Indradhanush 2014 mein, jo imyunaijeshan se related tha. ayushman Bharat 2018 mein, aur Prime Minister garib kalyan paikej 2020 mein. to ye saari ki saari yaad rakhni hain.

3 · kaushal aur vittiya samaveshanSkill & financial inclusion

ab skill development aur entaraprenyoraship se related bhi question baar-baar poochhe jate hain. question bahut hi simple rehte hain — skill development mishan ya kainpen kab aaya? ye 2015 yaad rakhna hai. Ministry of skill development end entaraprenyoraship ke under aata hai. atal innovation mishan flagship scheme hai, basically NITI Aayog ki. aur varld youth skill de kab manaya jata hai? 15 July ko.

ab phainenshiyal inklujan end soshal security scheme — agar main bolun na, jitni bhi schemes hum logon ne ki, usmein se sabse important ye wali hone wali hai. Prime Minister janadhan yojana 2014 mein — jaise hi government aati hai, mek in India — bhaiya government nayi-nayi bani hai 2014 mein to kya karenge? pehle to sabke akaunt mein paise bhej do. uske baad Bharat ko self-reliant banaayenge.

uske baad yahan pe stori suno — beti jo hai na, seks ratio jo hai vo achha nahi tha Haryana mein, to panipat se start kar diya Beti Bachao Beti Padhao. start karte hi ek ladki paida ho gayi jiska naam hai smriddhi — yaani sukanya smriddhi ho gaya, 2015 mein. ab usko rehne ke liye ghar bhi chahiye — Prime Minister Awaas yojana, 2015 mein hi. ghar milne ke baad usko chahiye ki uski suraksha bani rahe — to insurance bhi de diya. phir usko bijanes karna hai, bijanes ke liye loan chahiye — to currency bank. aur ganv mein bachapan mein padhne ke liye ilektrisiti bhi chahiye — rular ilektriphikeshan, dinadayal upadhyay gram jyoti yojana.

Atal Pension Yojana bhi 2015 mein hai. dekho kitni saari 2015 mein — amrit bhi 2015 mein hi hai, sitiz life ko betar banane ke liye. sugamy Bharat abhiyan yaani eksesibal India bhi 2015 mein hi hai, divyangajanon ke liye. Prime Minister ujjvala yojana ab 2016 ki aa gayi. crop insurance yojana 2016 mein, ayushman 2018 mein, jal jeevan mishan 2019 mein.

to 2015 mein kitni saari schemes thin — aur PCS jo hai na ghuma phira ke yahin se question puchhata rehta hai. isliye saare pivaikyu ko basically notes form mein kanvart kar diya, aur kuch nahi hai.
