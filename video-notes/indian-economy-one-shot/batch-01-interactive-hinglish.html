02

economy kya hai?What is an economy · scarcity & choice

§
paribhashaeconomy ek aisa system hai jismein desh utpadan (produce), vitaran (distribute) aur upbhog (consume) — teeno kaarya vastuon aur sevaon (goods & services) ka karta hai.

economy ki avdharna ka mool simit sansadhan (limited resources) arthat abhav / kami (scarcity) hai — kyunki sansadhan simit hain, isliye tay karna padta hai ki kis cheez ko praathmikta di jaaye.

kya utpadan karein
What to produce
kaise utpadan karein
How to produce
kiske liye utpadan karein
For whom to produce

yahi teen sawaal “kendriya samasya (central problem)” hain — inhi ka uttar economy ki sanrachna tay karta hai.

vargikaran ka aadhar

economy ka vargikaran samanyatah is aadhar par hota hai — utpadan ke sadhanon ka swamitva (ownership of means of production) evam unmein sarkar ki bhoomika.

  • utpadan ke sadhan = bhoomi, shram, poonji aur udyam (Land, Labour, Capital, Enterprise).
  • sawaal ye: ye sadhan kiske pass hain — niji vyakti, rajya (State), ya dono?
  • doosra sawaal: sarkar kitni sakriya bhoomika nibhati hai — niyantran/kalyanakari hastakshep ya keval niyam-nirdharan?
i
pariksha sanket“economy kya hai” — ye seedhe sawaal shayad hi aaye, par scarcity → choice → central problems aur ownership-based classification par aadharit sawaal baar-baar bante hain.

04

economy ke prakaarCapitalist · Socialist · Mixed · Gandhian

kram yaad rakhein: poonjivadi → samajvadi → mishrit → Gandhivadi/samajvadi. pehle teen “swamitva” ke aadhar par, chautha “decentralisation” ke aadhar par.

1. poonjivadi economy (Capitalist Economy)

  • utpadan ke sadhan niji swamitva mein — badi companies ke pass.
  • labh-unmukh (profit oriented) model; bazaar ki shaktiyan (market forces) keemat tay karti hain.
  • sarkar ki bhoomika nyoonatam/nishkriya — mahangai niyantran jaise sakriya hastakshep nahi.
  • udaharan: USA.

2. samajvadi economy (Socialist Economy)

  • sadhanon par rajya (State) ka swamitva; economic shakti centralised (centralized).
  • economic nirnay jan-kalyan ke liye — munafa sarvopari nahi.
  • udaharan: yadi bazaar mein vastuein itni mahangi ho jaayein ki garib varg khareed na sake, to sarkar hastakshep kar sasta/sulabh banati hai — yahi samajvadi vyavastha ki pehchan.

3. mishrit economy (Mixed Economy)

  • poonjivad + samajvad ka mel — niji (private) aur sarvajanik (public) dono kshetra saath-saath.
  • Bharat mishrit economy ka udaharan hai — ek or Tata, Ambani jaisi niji companies; doosri or PSU (sarvajanik kshetra).
  • saath hi kalyanakari (samajvadi) tatva: rashtriya khadya suraksha adhiniyam (NFSA) ke antargat lagbhag 80 crore logon ko riyayati dar (subsidised rate) par khadyann.
  • sarvajanik kshetra ka sanchalan rajya karta hai, niji kshetra ka vyakti/niji nikay.
!
Trap — “State” ka arthyahan “State” ka matlab Haryana/Punjab/UP jaisa rajya nahi, balki sanvidhan ke Article 12 mein paribhashit “rajya” hai — jismein sarkar, karyapalika, vidhayika aadi aate hain.

4. Gandhivadi samajvad (Gandhian Socialism)

  • aadhar: utpadak sansadhanon ka decentralisation (decentralization of productive resources) — sansadhan janata mein bante jaayein, kuch logon mein kendrit na hon.
  • jor swarozgar (self-employment), aatmanirbharta (self-reliance) aur sthaniya/gram-stariy utpadan par.
  • vyavaharik sanket: pichhle kuch varshon mein sarkar ka aatmanirbharta par focus isi vichar ki pratidhvani hai (jaise atmanirbhar Bharat).

developed · developing · underdevelopedDeveloped · Developing · Underdeveloped economies

Bharat
developing — developed nahi
USA, France
developed ke udaharan
sv-ghoshna
darja tay karne ka aadhar

Bharat developing economy hai — “developed” nahi. developed = USA, France jaise desh; Bharat abhi vikas ki prakriya mein hai. Bharat mein underdeveloped economy ke bhi kuch lakshan maujud hain, par sawaal mein vikalp diye hon to uttar developing hi hoga.

darja kaun deta hai? — Self-declaration

  • koi desh “developed” ya “developing” hai — ye atm-ghoshna (self-declaration) se tay hota hai; koi bahri pramanapatr anivary nahi.
  • labh: swayan ko developing ghoshit karne par kuch atirikt suvidhaen/chhoot milti hain — jaise lakshya prapti ke liye zyada samay (deadline mein chhoot): developed deshon ke liye 2050 to developing ke liye 2070 jaisi avadhi.
✎
sudhaar / sateektavideo mein ye “2050 banam 2070” ka udaharan samanya roop se diya gaya hai (kisi vishesh sandhi ka ullekh nahi). pariksha mein ise “avadhi/lakshya mein chhoot” ke udaharan ke roop mein hi likhein, kisi sandhi-vishesh ka dawa na karein.

underdeveloped economy ke lakshan (Features of Underdeveloped Economy)

lakshanspashtikaran
jeevan-star nimnStandard of living = kaise rehte hain, kya khaate hain, kahan rehte hain, kaisa paani/hawa milti hai — ye sab nimn star ke.
utpadakta nimnProductivity kam → aay kam → per capita income bhi kam.
jansankhya vriddhi tivrshiksha/jagarukta kam → parivar niyojan (family planning) ka abhav → uchch jansankhya vriddhi.
berozgari uchchuchch berozgari + alp-rozgar (underemployment).
krishi ka prabhutvkrishi (primary) kshetra ka dabadaba jitna zyada, economy utni pichhadi/underdeveloped; tertiary (seva) aur manufacturing ka hissa jitna zyada, utni unnat.
poonji nirman nimnkamaya aur kha liya, nivesh nahi kiya. Capital formation = machines/punjigat vastuon mein nivesh.
aay asamantaIncome inequality spasht dikhati hai.
nirbharta anupaat uchchDependency ratio uncha; karyashil jansankhya (workforce) mukhyatah prathamik/krishi kshetra mein.

developing economy ke lakshan (Features of Developing Economy)

  • vyavsay mukhyatah krishi — par GDP mein yogdan kam, nirbharta zyada.
  • prachhann berozgari (Disguised unemployment) — ek vyakti ka kaam chaar log kar rahe hain; teen hata dein to bhi utpadan par fark nahi.
  • human capital ki nimn gunavatta — kaushal, shiksha aur swasthya ka abhav.
  • audyogik aur takniki pichhadapan, mishrit economy, aayat par zyada nirbharta (aayat > niryaat), nirakshatra.
✎
sudhaar / sateekta — protein ka bindulecture mein kaha gaya: “uchch per person protein upalabdhata developed economy ka lakshan hai”. video mein ise “developing” kaha gaya, jo ulajha hua hai — sahi arth: protein-sanpannata developed deshon ka lakshan; Bharat protein-kami wala desh hai (shakahariyon ki baat to chhodie, mansahari bhi roz zaruri protein nahi le pate).
!
Trapunderdeveloped aur developing ke lakshan lagbhag saman dikhate hain — dono mein bhram na karein. pehchan: sawaal mein “Bharat” poocha jaaye → developing; kisi samanya/saiddhantik economy ki baat ho → underdeveloped ke lakshan chunein.

visheshataon ki soochi

dushchakra (Vicious circle) — shram utpadakta

  • shram ki utpadakta kam → aay kam.
  • aay kam → na swasthya, na kaushal, na anya nivesh.
  • isliye poonji nirman ki dar bhi nimn — yahi chakra garibi ko doharata hai.
§

paryavaraniya Kuznets curve (Environmental Kuznets Curve — EKC)

EKC paryavaraniya ksharan (environmental degradation) aur per person GDP ke beech sambandh dikhati hai: aay badhane ke saath ksharan pehle badhta hai, ek bindu ke baad ghatane lagata hai — isliye curve ulta-U (inverted U) aakaar ka hota hai.

✎

SDG ki kahani — 17 lakshya ek hi kahani mein

  6. haath-munh dhoya → swachh jal aur swachhata (Goal 6)
  7. school mein solar painal → sasti aur swachh urja (Goal 7)
  8. pass hokar achhi naukri → uchit kaarya aur economic vriddhi (Goal 8)
  9. naukri udyog mein mili → udyog, navachar aur avsanrachna (Goal 9)
  10. samaj mein izzat badhi → asamanta mein kami (Goal 10)
  11. badi city mein bas gaye → satat shahar aur samuday (Goal 11)
  12. chizon ka soch-samajhakar upyog → jimmedar upbhog aur utpadan (Goal 12)
  13. phir bhi jalvayu ka prakop → jalvayu karravai (Goal 13)
  14. baadh se jaliy jeevan (Goal 14) khatam
  15. aur thaliy jeevan (Goal 15) bhi khatam
  16. tay kiya shanti se rahenge → shanti, nyay aur sashakt sansthayein (Goal 16)
  17. sab milkar → lakshyon ke liye saajhedari (Goal 17)

human capital aur inclusive growthHuman capital · sources · inclusive growth

§
human capitalvo janashakti jo kushal (skilled), swasth (healthy) aur shikshit (educated) ho — yahi utpadakta badhati hai.

human capital nirman ke source (Sources of human capital formation)

inclusive growth

  • arth = “sabka saath, sabka vikas” — samaj ke sabhi vargon ki bhagidari aur dekhbhal.
  • ye bahuayami (multidimensional) avdharna hai — shiksha, swasthya, rozgar, garibi unmulan, har ayam mein sudhaar.
