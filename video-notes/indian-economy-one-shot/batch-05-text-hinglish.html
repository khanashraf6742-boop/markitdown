1 · inflation, deflation, disaiphleshan, reflationFour basic terms

chalo, ye hum ab aage ki baat karte hain. next hai hamara inflation. inflation kya hota hai? ki pehle koi cheez ₹100 ki thi, ab ₹104 ki hogi — mahangai hogi. inflation ka matlab mahangai.

deflation kya hota hai? ₹100 ki cheez ₹98 ki hogi — deflation. disaiphleshan kya hota hai? pehle koi ₹100 ki cheez thi, vo ₹104 ki hogi, phir vo ₹112 ki ho gayi, phir vo ₹108 ki hogi. matlab ki mera simple sa matlab ye hai — disaiphleshan ka matlab ye hai ki jo inflation hai na, mahangai to ho rahi hai, par uska jo rate hai na vo kam ho gaya. let's se pehle 10% inflation tha, phir 4% se badh raha hai, phir 2% se — to yaani ki jo rate of inflation hai, that is dikrijing.

reflation kya hota hai? ki government policies ki wajah se jo demand hai, usko rivaiv karne ki koshish ki jati hai. jaise abhi karentali dekhoge, GST ke jo rets hain vo kat kar diye gaye — kyun? taaki phir se economy rivaiv kare, taaki log zyada demand karein. agar demand karenge to obvious si baat hai, matlab achhi cheez rahegi. to ye reflation hota hai.

2 · stagflation, hyper aur tivrata ke starStagflation & degrees of inflation

staphleshan kya hota hai? ye vo situation hoti hai ki jab staigent growth, high inflation aur high unemployment rate ho. ek to growth staigent hogi, high inflation hai — yaani ki mahangai bahut zyada hai — plus mein high unemployment hai. logon ke pass paisa bhi nahi hai, inflation bhi high hai, growth bhi staigent ho gayi.

hyper inflation kya hota hai? koi ₹100 ki cheez thi, vo let se ₹10 ki ho gayi — sidha 60% inflation, vo ho gaya hyper. matlab bhai, inflation bilkul out of control ho gaya.

creeping inflation kya hota hai? mild inflation. end this is veri, you no, nesesri for e economic growth — economic growth ke liye kaafi zyada zaroori bhi hai.

walking inflation kya hota hai? ek modaret inflation, 3 se 10% ke beech mein. running inflation 10 se 20% ke beech mein — yaani koi ₹100 ki cheez ₹114 ki hogi, to this is running inflation. galloping inflation kya hota hai? kaafi high inflation, generally 20 se 30%.

3 · kor, headline aur kaaranCore, headline & causes

kor inflation kya hota hai? dekho, mana jata hai food aur phyul ke jo praises hain na, vo kaafi zyada voletail hote hain. to agar hum food aur phyul ke praises ko ghata ke inflation mejar karein, to usko bolte hain kor inflation — ki asli mahangai itni hai. kyunki yaar, jo phyul praises hote hain na vo hamari government pe dipend nahi karta. kabhi-kabhi kya hota hai — let's se USA ne iran pe senshan dal diya, ki bhai koi oil nahi kharidega, to ekadam se oil ke prais badh jaayenge. sidhi si baat hai, ye hum pe to dipend nahi kar raha na.

headline inflation kya hai? yahan par saare aitam overall jo inflation dekha jata hai na, wahi headline inflation hota hai.

next ho gaya aapka demand pul inflation. aisa inflation jo excessive demand ki wajah se — matlab koi partikular goods tha, jaise sainitaijar tha kovid ke time pe, ekadam se log sainitaijar kharidne lage. uski demand badh gayi, demand ki wajah se kya ho gaya na — uske praises bhi badh gaye. daits all.

kost push inflation — inflation raij hua hai kyunki jo kost of production hai na vo badh gaya. ki salary badh gayi, let's se ro materiyal mahanga ho gaya — uski wajah se kya hua na, jo production ka kost tha vo badh gaya, to obvious si baat hai, final prais jo hoga product ka, vo bhi badh jaayega.

next hai inported inflation — kyunki bhai, jo raij in prais of import hai, import ke jo prais hain usmein badhotri hogi. imagine a sineriyo — let's se aap aisa samajh low ki tel pehle bhai ₹100 ka aa raha tha, ab ₹120 ka aa raha hai. agar tel mahanga ho gaya, kyunki bahar se aa raha hai, to jitne bhi cheezein hain — dudh, dahi, sabji se le saari cheezon tak — kyunki saari cheezein transaport use karti hain na, trak wagairah, idhar se udhar — saari cheezein mahangi ho jaengi automatically.

strakcharal inflation kya hota hai? jab economy mein koi strakcharal problem ho. ok. opan inflation kya hota hai? to next hai guys sapresd inflation — yaani ki inflation hidan dyu to prais control, reshaning, blaik market. matlab ki bhai, inflation to hai, par usko kahin na kahin aisa dikhaya ja raha hai ki vo inflation hai hi nahi.

phir inflation kya hai? ki vej prais spayaral ki wajah se — ki varkar ne bola hamari salary badhao. salary badha di. ab salary higher, production kost higher, praises agen higher. matlab ki hua kya na — jab varkar ne bola hamari salary badhao, to uske baad jo production kost thi vo zyada ho gayi. jab production kost zyada ho jaegi to jo cheez thi market mein vo mahangi ho jaegi. kyunki salary jo badhai hai, kahin na kahin jo malik hai vo kahin se to paise nikalega na — to usne jo biskit pehle ₹10 ka bana tha, vo ₹12 ka bechane laga. ab isi biskit ko kaun khareed rahe hain? isi biskit ko ye bande khareed rahe hain. ab inko laga yahan pe bhi dikkat ho gayi — bhai, matlab ₹12 ka biskit ho gaya, phir se ye salary demand raij karte hain, to spayaral ban jata hai baar-baar.

4 · inaphleshanari gap, philips curve, indexGaps, Phillips curve & price indices

inaphleshanari gap — let's se aapki jo poori economy hai, full employment pe jo hai vo ₹100 kama sakte hain, lekin kisi saal aapki economy ne ₹120 kama liya, to jo ₹120 hai na, ye extra jo hai, ye because of inaphleshanari gap.

philips curve kya hai? ye dikhati hai inavars relationship inflation aur unemployment ke beech mein, short ran mein. yaani ki agar inflation badhega to unemployment ghatega, agar unemployment ghatega to inflation badhega. ye inavars relationship hai — yaani ki high inflation, low employment. normal si cheez hai.

prais indeks — consumer prais indeks kya hota hai? dekho, ye vo inflation hota hai jo consumer ke dwara eksapiriyans kiya jata hai. alag-alag hote hain — jaise sipiai rular, arban, kanbaind sipiai hai, agriculture labour, industrial varkar. ab jo ye agriculture labour aur industrial varkar hai na, inka jo bes prais tha vo bhi chenj kiya gaya hai — vo aapko current apheyar mein dekhne ko milega.

chalo next aapka holasel prais indeks — yaani ki jo prais chenj hua hai holasel level pe. GDP deflator main already bata chuka hoon — nominal GDP apon riyal GDP × 100, ye aapka inflation mejar karne ka vo hai. prodyusar prais indeks kya hai? ye inflation ko mejar karta hai prodyusar aur industry level pe.

indekseshan kya hota hai? indekseshan simple sa matlab hota hai ki jaise-jaise inflation badhe, to aapki jo salary hai vo bhi uske hisab se badhti jaaye.

diphleshanari gap kya hota hai? ki bhai, aapka jo full employment level pe — let's se aapki potenshiyal thi ₹100 ki — aap full employment pe kaam karke bhi sirf ₹90 hi kama paaye, to diphleshanari gap ho gaya.
