01

balance of payments (BoP) ka infrastructureBalance of Payments · current & capital account

BoP ka matlab sirf itna hai — poori duniya ke saath hamara hisab: kiske saath deficits mein hain, kiske saath fayde mein. baqi sab isi ki shakhaen hain.

§
do khaate, bas do Current account = merchandise (goods ka export–import) + invisibles (services, factor income, transfers).
Capital account = bahar se poonji ka ana-jana — FDI/FPI, external commercial borrowing, NRI deposits.

Trap — tariff aur drawbackTariff = seema par karte saman par tax, jo domestic industry ko bachane ke liye lagata hai (danping ke samne). ₹50 ka desi saman, chin ₹20 ka de raha → government ₹30 tax lagakar dono ko 50-50 par le aati hai.
Duty drawback = niryatak ko import duty ka refund.

02

rupees ki parivartaniyta aur vinimay rateConvertibility · exchange rate · devaluation · FEMA 1999

yahan zyadatar galati depreciation aur devaluation mein hoti hai — farq sirf itna hai ki kaun gira raha hai.

§
parivartaniyta (convertibility) Current account — poori convertibility, 1994 mein hasil. aap jitna chahe currency badalava lein — koi seema nahi.
Capital account — poori nahi; bahar loan lena, videsh mein property kharidna — in par seema hai. isi ke liye tarapur committee bani, jisne sharten tay kin ki kab poori hogi.

population ke siddhantMalthus · optimum population · demographic transition

teeno siddhanton ke sirf naam aur ek-ek lain poochhe jate hain — isliye naam-varsh pehle pakka karein.

siddhantkisne diyamool baat
Malthusian theoryT.R. Malthus — “An Essay on the Principle of Population”, 1798population geometric (2, 4, 8, 16) badhti hai, khana arithmetic (2, 3, 4, 5) → khane wale zyada, khana kam. hal: birth control
Optimum population theoryEdwin Cannansansadhanon ke hisab se sabse behatar abadi
Social adjustment theoryHenry Georgesamajik adjustment se abadi ka samanjasy
Demographic transition theoryThompsonchaar stej — birth aur death rate ka utar-appreciation
§
chaar stej, ek lain mein1. Birth high + death high → abadi sthir (purane zamane mein 8 bachche, 4 bachate) · 2. Death rate gira (aspatal, vaiksin), birth high → population explosion · 3. Birth rate bhi girne laga, abadi phir bhi badh rahi · 4. dono low → phir sthir.
i
Bharat kis stej meinBharat tisri stej mein hai — “high growth with a sign of slowing down”. birth rate gir raha hai, par abadi badh rahi hai.
§
teen aur paribhashaenNatural growth = birth − death; ismein migration aur marriage nahi gine jate.
Stable population = jahan birth aur death rate sthir/barabar hon.
Demographic features = density, sex ratio, literacy, age structure, rural–urban distribution. Standard of living ismein nahi aata — ye trap hai.
