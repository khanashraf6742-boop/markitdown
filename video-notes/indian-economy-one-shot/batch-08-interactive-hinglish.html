hissa ghatna achha hai ya bura?economic drishti se achha — theory kahati hai ki jab kheti mein lagi abadi ka percent ghatata hai, to iska matlab log dusre utpadak kshetron (industry, seva) mein kaam kar rahe hain. par kheti par dependent 46% abadi abhi bhi bahut badi hai.

tisri yojana mein rinatmak growth kyun?un varshon mein chin se yuddh (1962) aur Pakistan se yuddh (1965) chal rahe the, saath mein sukha. isliye 1966 ke baad Green Revolution, “jay javan jay kisan” ka slogan aur khadyann mein self-reliance par zor diya gaya — lal bahadur shastri ji ke samay.
!
Trap — rinatmak growth ka matlab“Negative growth” ka matlab utpadan ghatna hai — jaise pichhle saal 100 tan tha to is saal 98 tan. ye “growth slow hona” nahi, balki pichhe jana hai. kovid mein sabhi kshetron mein rinatmak growth thi, sirf agriculture ko chhodakar.

agriculture utpadon ki mang — inelastickhane-pine ki zaruri chizon ki mang inelastic hoti hai: chaval ₹50/kilo se ₹90/kilo ho jaaye, to family 10 kilo ki jagah 8–9 kilo kharidega — par kharidega zarur. isliye zyada utpadan hone par bhi kisan ki aay/munafa gadabada jata hai.
§
Bullock capitalistve kisan jo “possess means but are not rich” hain — self-employed aur self-financed. bullock cart se mandi tak mal le jate hain, munafe ko zamin-trade-bail mein lagate hain aur dhire-dhire chhote zamindar ban jate hain. ye shabd Lloyd aur Susanne Rudolph ne 1987 mein gadha.
!
Trap“kaun commercial crop hai?” mein alu, ganna, kapas, repasid — charon commercial hain. gehun-chaval ko food crop mana jata hai, isliye unhen commercial ki List mein na likhein.
03

bhoomi reform aur jot ka aakaarLand reforms & land holding categories

bhoomi reform ke chaar-panch hisse hain, aur jot (holding) ki panch shreniyan — dono par direct question bante hain.

§
bhoomi reform ke ghatak 1. zamindari unmulan — middlemen (intermediaries) hatana · 2. bhoomi jot par siling — ek vyakti maximum itni hi zamin rakh sakata hai · 3. Tenancy reform — kiraye par zamin lene valon (tenants) ke niyam · 4. Security of tenure — kitne samay ke liye kiraya tay hai · 5. Ownership on tenant — tenant ko malikana haq milega ya nahi.
shrenijot ka aakaar

Small1–2 hekteyar
Semi-medium2–4 hekteyar
Medium4–10 hekteyar
Large10 hekteyar se zyada
§
Small aur marginal = 89%Economic Survey ke anusar small aur marginal kisan milkar lagbhag 89% hain — isliye dono ko ek saath “small & marginal” kaha jata hai. Semi-medium aur medium zyadatar Punjab-Haryana jaise kshetron mein dikhate hain, aur large lagbhag nagany hai.
§
agriculture ki utpadakta kam kyun? 1. bhoomi par population ka dabav · 2. Disguised unemployment — ek chhote kaam par chaar log lage hain; teen ko hata dein to bhi koi farq nahi padega · 3. jot ka aakaar chhota — bade measure (economies of scale) ka fayda nahi · 4. inhi reasons se rural poverty bhi bani rehti hai, kyunki sabse zyada kisan ganvon mein rehte hain aur ganv kheti par dependent hai.
✦
ek lain meinpoverty hatani hai to jis sector mein log sabse zyada lage hain, use behatar banao ya uska vikalp do — lecture ka yahi formula hai.

rashan card par kiska naam?family ki sabse umradaraz mahila jiski ayu 18 varsh ya zyada ho, wahi head of household mani jati hai aur rashan card usi ke naam hota hai. women aur bachchon ke nutrition par vishesh dhyan diya gaya, kyunki hidden hunger ki samasya hai.
§
khadya suraksha ke ayam (3A + 2)Availability — grain uplabdh ho · Accessibility — pahunch mein ho (40 kimi door na jana pade) · Affordability — qimat vahaniy ho · iske alava Utilization aur Stability bhi ayam hain.
