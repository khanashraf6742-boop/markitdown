01

inflation kya hai?Inflation · deflation · disinflation · reflation

ye chapter chaar shabdon ke farq par khada hai — inflation, deflation, disinflation, reflation. pariksha mein inhi charon ko aapas mein ulat-palat kar poocha jata hai.

§
inflationjab prices badhti jaayein. lecture ka sidha udaharan: pehle koi cheez ₹100 ki thi, ab ₹104 ki ho gayi — yaani inflation ho gayi.
shabdkeemat ka hallecture ka udaharan
Inflation
inflation		 prices badh rahi hain ₹100 → ₹104
Deflation
deflation		 prices ghat rahi hain ₹100 → ₹98
Disinflation
raftar ghatna		 inflation ho rahi hai, par uski raftar ghat rahi hai ₹100 → ₹104 → ₹112 → ₹108 (rate ghati)
Reflation
punahsphiti		 government/policy ki madad se mang ko phir se zinda karna GST ki daren ghatai gain taaki log zyada khariden
!
Trap — deflation banam disinflationDeflation mein prices neeche jati hain (₹100 → ₹98). Disinflation mein prices upar hi jati hain, bas badhane ki rate slow ho jati hai. dono ko ek samajhna sabse am galati hai.
i
Reflation kab?jab economy sust ho aur government chahe ki log kharch karein — kar ghatana, kharch badhana, byaj rate ghatana. lecture mein yahi udaharan diya: “GST ke rets kat kar diye, taaki economy rivaiv ho aur log zyada demand karein.”
02

tivrata ke aadhar par prakaarCreeping → walking → running → galloping → hyper

inflation ki gati dekhakar naam tay hota hai — slow se lekar bekabu tak.

prakaarrate (lecture anusar)matlab
creepingbahut kam / mildhalki inflation — economic growth ke liye zaruri bhi hai
walking3%–10%chalati hui inflation
running10%–20%₹100 ki cheez ₹114 ki ho jaaye
galloping20%–30%bahut tez, daudti hui inflation
hyperpoori tarah bekabu“inflation bilkul out of control”
§
stagflationteen chizen ek saath: sust growth (stagnant growth) + tez inflation (high inflation) + unchi unemployment (high unemployment).
“logon ke pass paisa bhi nahi, inflation bhi high, aur growth bhi ruki hui” — yahi sabse bura sanyojan hai.
!
TrapStagflation ≠ sirf inflation. ismein teeno sharten zaruri hain — growth stagnant, inflation high, unemployment high. ek bhi chhuti to uttar badal jaayega.
✎
reformlecture mein haiparainaphleshan ka udaharan bolte samay ankada gadabada gaya (“₹100 ki cheez ₹10 ki ho gayi, 60%”). manak paribhasha: haiparainaphleshan = inflation poori tarah niyantran se bahar (bahut tez, mahine-rate-mahine). pariksha mein “out of control” wali paribhasha hi likhein.
03

inflation ke kaaranDemand-pull · cost-push · imported · structural · wage-price spiral

§
demand-pul (Demand-pull)mang zyada ho jane se keemat badhna.
lecture ka udaharan: kovid ke samay achanak sabane sanitizer kharidna shuru kiya → mang badhi → keemat badh gayi.
§
kost-push (Cost-push)utpadan ki lagat badhane se keemat badhna.
jaise salary badh gayi ya kachcha mal mahanga ho gaya → lagat badhi → final prais bhi badha.
§
importedbahar se aane wali cheez mahangi ho jaaye.
udaharan: tel pehle ₹100 ka aa raha tha, ab ₹120 ka — to transaport mahanga, aur dudh-dahi-sabzi sab mahangi.
§
strakcharal (Structural)economy ki structural samasya (jaise apurti shrrinkhala ki kamazori) se paida inflation.
✦
vej-prais spairal (Wage-Price Spiral)mazadur bole “salary badhao” → salary badhi → lagat badhi → biskit ₹10 se ₹12 ka ho gaya → ab mazadur ko phir laga ki inflation badh gayi, salary badhao → chakra chalata rehta hai. ise hi vej-prais spairal kehte hain.
!
Trap — pehchan kaise kareinagar kahani mein “log zyada kharidne lage” hai → demand-pull. agar “lagat/kachcha mal/salary badhi” hai → cost-push. agar “bahar se aaya saman mahanga hua” hai → imported.
04

khuli aur dabi inflationOpen vs suppressed inflation

khuli inflation (Open)
prices khule bazaar mein svatantr roop se badhti hain — government unhen rok nahi rahi
dabi inflation (Suppressed)
inflation hai, par keemat niyantran, rashaning aur kalabazar ke kaaran dikhati nahi — “chhipa di gayi inflation”

lecture ki sidhi baat: “bhai inflation to hai, par usko kahin na kahin aisa dikhaya ja raha hai ki vo inflation hai hi nahi.”

i
teeno saath kyun aate haingovernment keemat tay kar deti hai (price control) → cheez kam pad jati hai (rationing) → jo chahiye vo zyada dam par bahar milta hai (black market). isliye suppressed inflation ka doosra naam “chhipi hui inflation” hai — aankde kam dikhate hain, asar wahi rehta hai.
!
Trapquestion mein agar “price niyantran / rashan / kalabazar” shabd aen, to uttar suppressed inflation hai — open nahi.
3
sanket — price control, rationing, black market
0
ankadon mein dikhane wali inflation (dabi hui)
100%
asar jeb par wahi
05

gap aur philips curveInflationary gap · deflationary gap · Phillips curve

§
inflationary gappoorn employment (full employment) par economy jitna kama sakti thi usse zyada kama leti hai.
lecture ka udaharan: kshamta ₹100 ki thi, kamaya ₹120 → ₹20 ka extra = inflationary gap.
§
apasphitik gap (Deflationary Gap)poorn employment par kshamta ₹100 ki thi, par kamaya sirf ₹90 → ₹10 ka ghata = deflationary gap.
gapsthitiasar
Inflationary gapkshamta se zyada utpadan/kharchprices badhti hain
Deflationary gapkshamta se kam utpadan/kharchunemployment aur susti badhti hai
§
philips curve (Phillips Curve)short-term (short run) mein inflation aur unemployment ke beech ulta sambandh (inverse relationship).
inflation badhe → unemployment ghate; unemployment ghate → inflation badhe. “high inflation, low anaenployament.”
!
Trapphilips curve ka sambandh ulta hai, sidha nahi — aur ye short-term ke liye hai. long-term mein ye sambandh nahi mana jata.
✦
dono gap ek lain meinInflationary = zyada kharch (₹120 vs ₹100) · Deflationary = kam kharch (₹90 vs ₹100). bas tulna ₹100 (kshamta) se karani hai.
06

price indexCPI · WPI · PPI · GDP deflator

inflation kisi ek number se nahi napi jati — alag-alag index alag-alag star ki prices dekhate hain.

indexkis star ki priceskise inflation lagati hai
CPI (Consumer Price Index)khudra (retail) — jo grahak chukata haiam consumer
WPI (thok price index)thok (wholesale) starvyapari/utpadak
PPI (utpadak price index)utpadak aur industry starutpadak
GDP deflatorpoori economysab — par sabse chauda maap

CPI ke prakaar

  • CPI-gramin · CPI-shahari · CPI-sanyukt (combined)
  • CPI-agriculture shramik (AL) aur CPI-industrial shramik (IW) — inka aadhar varsh badala gaya hai, ye karent affairs mein poocha jata hai.
i
kaun-sa sabse zarurimaudrik policy (RBI) mukhyatah CPI-sanyukt ko dekhati hai, kyunki vo am aadmi ki inflation dikhata hai. WPI ki ganna vanijy mantralay karta hai, CPI ki sankhyiki mantralay (MoSPI).
✎
reform / sateektalecture mein CPI ke kuch rupon ko “inflation” kah diya gaya. dhyan rakhein — CPI ek index hai, inflation usse nikali jati hai (CPI ki growth rate = inflation).
07

inflation target aur indekseshanInflation targeting · indexation

§
inflation lakshyikaran (Inflation Targeting)thodi inflation growth ke liye zaruri hai, isliye RBI ek target rakhata hai.
4% ± 2% — yaani inflation 2% se 6% ki renj mein rahani chahiye.
4%
target inflation rate
±2%
upar-neeche ki chhoot
2–6%
svikary renj
i
video ke bahar se, par pariksha mein aata haiye target RBI adhiniyam mein 2016 ke sanshodhan ke baad tay hota hai; ise government RBI se salah karke tay karti hai aur maudrik policy committee (MPC) — 6 members — is par nirnay leti hai. lecture mein sirf “RBI ka target 4% ± 2%” bataya gaya.
§
indekseshan (Indexation)“jaise-jaise inflation badhe, waise-waise salary bhi badhti jaaye.” isse karmachari ki asli kray shakti bachi rehti hai — khasakar sarkari karmachariyon ke DA (inflation bhatta) mein yahi tark hai.
sthitiRBI am taur par kya karta hai
inflation 6% se uparbyaj daren badhata hai (inflation par lagam)
inflation 2% se neeche / sustibyaj daren ghatata hai (mang badhane ke liye)
2–6% ke beech“aram ki sthiti” — balance banaye rakhata hai
!
Traptarget 4% hai, “4 se 6” nahi. ±2% ka matlab hai 2% se 6% ki renj — question mein aksar “4–6%” likhakar galat vikalp banaya jata hai.
✦
is chapter ka sar ek lain meininflation badhna = inflation · ghatna = deflation · rate ka ghatna = disinflation · mang jagana = reflation · teeno bure saath = stagflation.
