01

HDI aur khushhali ke indexHuman Development Index · GNH

“Bharat ki rank kitni hai?” — ye sawal har saal aata hai. HDI ke teen aayam aur navintam rank, dono zabani yaad hone chahiye.

§
HDI ke teen aayam 1. Health — janm ke samay jeevan pratyasha (life expectancy at birth) · 2. Education — average aur apekshit skuli varsh (mean & expected years of schooling) · 3. Standard of living — per capita income (GNI per capita).
130
Bharat ki rank (HDR 2025)
193
kul desh
3
HDI ke aayam
i
report ka shirshak bhi poocha jata haiHDR 2025 ka shirshak — “A Matter of Choice: People and Possibilities in the Age of AI”. lecture ki salah: “rank 130 hai, ye question chhapega — chhap gaya samajh low.”
§
Gross National Happiness (GNH)bhutan ne 1972 mein apanaya — tatkalin raja Jigme Singye Wangchuck. ye teen baton par aadharit hai — life satisfaction, life expectancy aur environment.
!
Trap — HDI kaun nikalta haiHDI UNDP ki Human Development Report mein aata hai — vishv bank ya IMF ki report mein nahi. aur HDI mein “khushi” ya “asamanta” aayam nahi hai; ve alag index hain.
✦
ek lain meinHEALTH + EDUCATION + LIVING STANDARD = HDI · 130/193 = Bharat · UNDP = jarikarta · GNH = bhutan 1972.

!

05

beti bachao, beti padhao aur bal kalyanBeti Bachao · Sukanya · ICDS

“Haryana mein sex ratio achha nahi tha, to panipat se shuruaat ki gayi” — is ek vakya mein do fact hain: 2015 aur panipat.

§
Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (2015)balika janm ko protsahan aur uski education. shuruaat panipat, Haryana se hui, kyunki wahan sex ratio kam tha.
§
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (2015)balika ke naam par saving khata — education aur vivah ke kharch ke liye. BBBP ke saath hi shuru hui, isliye dono 2015 mein yaad rakhein.
§
ICDS (1975) — jodne wali kadibal kalyan ki sabse purani yojana — anganavadi ke zariye bachchon, pregnant women aur mataon ko nutrition. janani suraksha (2005) aur janani shishu suraksha (2011) ise aage badhati hain.
✦
teen saal, ek sidhi1975 ICDS (bachcha) → 2005 JSY (man) → 2011 JSSK (man + shishu). iske upar 2015 mein BBBP aur sukanya.

10BoP

Current account (merchandise + invisibles), capital account, balance of trade, CAD.

10vinimay rate

Floating rate, convertibility (Tarapore), devaluation 1949 · 1966 · 1991, FEMA 1999.

10sansthayein

Bretton Woods 1944, World Bank grup ke 5 ang, IMF kota aur SDR, NDB, ADB 1966, AIIB 2016, BIS 1930.
