1 · Domestic TerritoryDomestic territory — embassies, ships, aircraft

to guys hum log baat kar rahe the Domestic Territory ki. let's se ye hai aapka India. India ke andar USA enbesi hai. ab USA enbesi kuch matlab profit kamati hai, to Bharat ki GDP mein add nahi hoga. kyunki generally jo phoren enbesi hoti hai na, vo jo jis jamin pe bani hui hai — aisa mana jata hai ki ye jo jamin hai na ye inhi ki hai.

jaise example ke taur pe ye USA hai, USA mein Indian enbesi hai. ab 15 August ko kuch phlaig sel karke Indian enbesi ne kuch paisa kamaya, to vo yahan USA ki GDP mein add nahi hoga — vo India ki GDP mein add hoga. kyunki kahin na kahin ye India ka hi part hai. samajh rahe ho? sem ye jo USA enbesi hai, kahin na kahin USA ka hi part hai. isliye bola jata hai, agar aapne kisi desh ki enbesi pe ataik kiya to ye mana jata hai ki aapne us desh pe ataik kar diya.

2 · factor income aur final gudFactor income & final vs intermediate goods

rejident ko factor income kya hoti hai? dekho, ek to hota hai aapka land, labour, capital, entaraprenyoraship. land se aapko kya milta hai bhai? land se aapko milta hai rent. labour ko kya milti hai? vejes. capital se intarest, entaraprenyor ko profit — kyunki vo risk le raha hai. to inhi ko aap plus karoge to aa jaayega factor income.

final gud kya hota hai? final gud hota hai jo ki aapka final production mein — matlab ki jo final consumption ke liye yuz hua hai. aisa gud jo ki final consumption ke parpas se paraches kiya gaya, usi ko final gud bolte hain. iski koi bhi risel ya phardar production nahi hoti.

example ke taur pe, maine ek markar kharida. ab ye jo markar maine kharida na, ye markar maine isliye kharida taaki main aap logon ko padha sakun — to ye intaramidiet gud ho jaayega, kyunki main isko final service dene ke liye yuz kar raha hoon. lekin main ye markar apne parsanal yuz ke liye kar raha hoon, to ye mera final gud ho jaayega. to jo final gud hai, intaramidiet gud hai — vo dipend karega aapke parpas pe.

3 · GDP, NDP, GNP, NNPThe core formulas

next aayega gros Domestic product. main already aapko bata chuka hoon — monitri value saari goods aur services ki, jo ek saal mein banati hai Domestic Territory ke andar. pharmula kya hota hai? private kansamashan plus investment plus government eksapendichar, uske baad plus karke export mainas import. ye kar do — ye aapka pharmula ho jaayega GDP ka.

net Domestic product kya hota hai? ab bhai kuch jo capital goodss hain unka viyar end tiyar bhi hoga, kuch na kuch unki jo deprisieshan hai vo bhi hogi. agar aap GDP mein se — kyunki ye to gros hai — deprisieshan mainas kar do to net aa jaayega. jaise aapki gros salary aur net salary mein farak hota hai, right?

jienpi kya hai? dekho, gros national product mein kya hai na — Domestic product mein kya tha ki bhai Bharat ki Territory ke andar jo bhi bana hua hai vo Bharat ki GDP mein add hoga, chahe vo USA wale banaen, chahe japan wale banaen, koi bhi banaye. lekin jab main gros national product ki baat karoon na, to gros national product mein basically yahan par neshanaliti maitar karti hai — yaani ki Bharat ke nagarik pure sansar mein kahin par bhi koi bhi production kar rahe hain, to vo hamari jienpi mein add hoga. to iska pharmula kya ho jaayega? GDP plus net factor income phrom abrod.

ab kuch log bolenge sir aap itna bhaag-bhaag ke kyun padha rahe ho? bhaiya main mairathan padha raha hoon. agar aapko isko ditel mein padhna hai na, do ghante ka lecture pada hua hai isi pe — poora achhe se ek-ek cheez ditel mein batai hui hai yupiessi Perspective se.

net factor income phrom abrod kya hote hain? ki bhai kuch aise bande hain jo ki phoren se aake India mein settle ho gaye, kuch aise jo India se phoren mein chale gaye — to aapas mein vo paisa kama rahe honge, to uska hum net iphekt dekhate hain.

net national product kya hai? jienpi mein se aap deprisieshan ghata do — wahi aa jaayega. ab national income et factor kost — dekho do-teen cheezein hoti hain: ek to hoti hai aapka factor kost, ek hota hai aapka market prais.

ab inko samajhna kaise hai? value of goods end services et e kost of production — kost of production matlab ki aapko total kitna paisa pada ye product dene mein. ismein aapka taiks, sabsidij vo sab inklud nahi hota. lekin agar aap taiks aur sabsidij inklud kar doge to phir vo market prais pe ban jaayega. simple si cheez hai.

4 · parsanal income, dispojebal income, per capitaPersonal & disposable income, per capita

next aapka parsanal income. ab parsanal income kya hoti hai? jo income ek individual ko risiv hoti hai, taiks se pehle. ismein vo saari cheezein eksaklud ho jati hain jo usko nahi milatin. jaise anadistribyuted korporet profit — bhai korporet profit individual ko thodi milega, vo to unke malikon ko milega. korporet taiks usko thodi milega, vo to government ko milega. soshal security kantribyushan — ye bhi kaun karta hai? wahi karte hain jo humein naukri dete hain, kyunki vo kuch contribute karte hain soshal security mein. to ye individual ko nahi milta, baad mein milta hai, kandishanal hota hai.

ab dispojebal parsanal income kya hogi? parsanal income mein se aap direct taiks mainas kar do — kyunki humein taiks bhi dena padega. vo aa gayi vo jo aapki jeb mein aayegi, jisako aap yuz kar sakte ho.

next aa gaya aapka per capita income. per capita income kya aa jaegi? total national income apon mein total population — yaani ki GDP apon mein total population. vo aapki aa jaegi per capita income, yaani ki evarej income kitni hai us nagarik ki.

ab aa jaayega aapka nominal income. nominal income kya hoti hai? vo income jo ki current Year ke praises pe aapko mejar ki jati hai — matlab ki wahan pe inflation ka iphekt nahi dikhaya jata. aur riyal income hoti hai jahan pe na inflation se edajast karke dikhaya jata hai.

5 · value eded, sarkular phlo, diphletarValue added, circular flow, GDP deflator

ab next aapka value edit. value edit kya hota hai? ki bhai value of output — let se ₹100 — mainas mein value of intaramidiet consumption. let se aapne ₹50 ka bed kharida, usko ₹100 mein bech gaya — to aapka jo value edishan hua vo ₹50 ka hua. sidhi si baat samajh mein aa gayi?

chalo next hai sarkular phlo of income. matlab ki yahi bata raha hai ki goods end services aur mani jo hai na, autasahold aur pharm ke beech mein aise ghumta hi rehta hai. ye aapka enasiarti mein ek dayagram bhi diya hua hai.

GDP diphletar kya hai? ye basically inflation ko mejar karta hai GDP ka use karke. bas itna hi yaad rakhna. grin GDP kya hai? ki aap GDP mein jo hai na environmental kost ko bhi inklud karte ho.

6 · rashtriya aay measure ki teen vidhiyanProduction, income & expenditure method

national income mejar karne ke na jaise GDP ko mejar karne ke teen methad hain — production methad, income methad, eksapendichar methad.

production methad mein aapne kitna value edishan kiya? income methad mein jo factor income hai — vej, rent, intarest, profit — inko plus kar diya jata hai. eksapendichar methad mein jitna bhi eksapendichar kiya jata hai, jo abhi humne upar dekha tha — consumption plus investment plus investment bay government plus export mainas import — ye wala. to ye ho gaye aapke teeno ke teeno methad.

dekho, jitna aapko nesesri hai na, sirf main utna hi padha raha hoon. ananesesri main ditel mein nahi ja raha hoon. theek hai? chalo, ye hum ab hum log aage ki baat karte hain — next hai hamara inflation.
