01

rashtriya aay ki buniyadDomestic territory · resident · factor income

ye chapter chhota hai, par sabse zyada number wala hai — yahan har sawal “GDP, GNP, NNP” ke formule par banta hai. lecture mein bhi saaf kaha gaya: “jitna zaruri hai, sirf utna hi padha raha hoon.”

§
shuruat yahan sepehle samjho domestic territory (gharelu kshetra) — kyunki GDP isi ke andar bani chizen ginta hai.
sthitikya Bharat ki GDP mein gina jaayega?kyun
Bharat mein bani ameriki dutavas ki imarat aur uski kamainahidutavas ki zamin us desh ka hissa mani jati hai — ye amerika ka sector hai
amerika mein Bharatiya dutavas ki kamaihanvo Bharat ka hissa hai — “dutavas par hamala = us desh par hamala”
Bharatiya jahaz, havai jahaz, machhali pakadane wale jahaz, samudri tel-pletaformhanye Bharat ke gharelu kshetra (domestic territory) mein gine jate hain
✦
yaad rakhne ki tarakibdutavas mein “jiski zamin, uski GDP”. Bharat mein ameriki dutavas ke kamare mein paisa kamaya — vo amerika ki GDP mein jaayega, Bharat ki nahi.

factor aay (Factor Income) — chaar sadhan, chaar aay

bhoomi / land
lagan (rent)
shram / labour
wage (wages)
poonji / capital
byaj (interest)
udyamashilta / entrepreneurship
labh (profit) — kyunki ye jokhim uthata hai

in charon ko jod do = factor aay. aur ye aay samanya nivasi (normal resident) ko milni chahiye — isliye “kaun nivasi hai” ye bhi dekha jata hai.

i
Transfer income vs factor incomefactor aay = kaam ke badale mili aay (vetan, lagan, byaj, labh). transfer income = bina kaam ke mili aay (penshan, chhatravritti, upahar) — ise rashtriya aay mein nahi joda jata.
02

antim aur intermediate goodenFinal vs intermediate goods · value added · circular flow

GDP mein sirf final gooden gini jati hain — varna ek hi cheez kai baar gini jaegi (double counting).

§
lecture ka udaharanmaine ek markar kharida. agar main usse padhata hoon, to vo intermediate good hai — kyunki vo aage seva dene mein istemal ho raha hai. par agar main use apne niji upyog ke liye rakh lun, to wahi markar final good ban jata hai.
!
Trapantim ya madhyavarti — ye cheez par nahi, upyog ke objective (purpose) par dependent hai. ek hi vastu do jagah do roop mein ho sakti hai.

price growth (Value Added)

formula
Value Added = Value of Output − Value of Intermediate Consumption
udaharan
₹50 ka bed kharida, ₹100 mein becha → price growth ₹50
fayda
har star par sirf “joda gaya price” ginne se double counting nahi hoti

03

GDP — paribhasha aur formulaGross Domestic Product & its expenditure formula

§
paribhashaGDP = ek varsh mein gharelu kshetra (domestic territory) ke andar bani sabhi vastuon aur sevaon ka bazaar price (monetary value).
i
sabse badi baatGDP mein sector mayne rakhata hai, citizenship nahi. Bharat ki seema ke andar ameriki company ne karakhana lagaya to uska utpadan Bharat ki GDP mein joda jaayega — chahe malik ameriki ho.

expenditure vidhi se formula (Expenditure Method)

=
GDP = C + I + G + (X − M)
C = niji upbhog (private consumption) · I = investment · G = government expenditure · X − M = export − import (net exports)
ghatakmatlabudaharan
Cgharon ka kharchroti, kapada, mobail, kiraya
Icapital kharchmachine, factory, nayi imarat
Ggovernment ka kharchsadak, salary, raksha
X − Mvidesh se net kamaiexport mein se import ghatao
!
Trap — transfer ginti mein nahipenshan, chhatravritti, unemployment bhatta GDP mein nahi jode jate — ye badale mein koi vastu/seva paida nahi karte. purani chizon ki bikri (second-hand) bhi nahi jodi jati.
04

GNP, NDP aur NNPGross/Net · Domestic/National · NFIA & depreciation

chaar chizen milkar poora khel banati hain: Gross/Net (depreciation) aur Domestic/National (citizenship).

conceptformulayaad rakhne ki baat
GDPdesh ki seema mein utpadansector aadharit
NDPGDP − depreciationNet = Gross se ghisai hatao
GNPGDP + NFIANFIA = videsh se net factor aay
NNPGNP − depreciationdono reform ek saath
NNP at Factor CostNNPMP − apratyaksh kar + subsidyyahi “rashtriya aay” hai
§
NFIA kya haiNet Factor Income from Abroad = Bharatiya citizens ki videsh se aay − videshiyon ki Bharat se aay.
“kuch log bahar se akar Bharat mein bas gaye, kuch Bharat se bahar chale gaye — dono ka net asar dekhate hain.”
i
GDP banam GNP — ek lain meinGDP mein sector mayne rakhata hai (Bharat ki seema mein jo bana), GNP mein citizenship (Bharatiya nagarik ne duniya mein kahin bhi banaya to Bharat ki GNP mein). isliye GNP = GDP + NFIA.
✦
“Gross vs Net” ko ghar ke udaharan seGross salary aur Net salary ka farq — beech mein jo katata hai (yahan machines ki ghisai) wahi depreciation hai. use ghatao to “Net” aa jata hai.
✎
reform / sateektalecture mein NNP ko kabhi “GNP mein se depreciation” to kabhi seedhe “GDP se” kaha gaya. sahi kram: GDP → (NFIA jodo) GNP → (depreciation ghatao) NNP. dono sudharon ka kram badalane se uttar nahi badalata, par pariksha mein formula theek vaisa hi likhein.
05

factor lagat banam bazaar keematFactor cost vs market price

ye pure chapter ka sabse zyada galat hone wala hissa hai — sirf ek formula yaad rakhna hai aur usmein kar/subsidy ka chinh (sign) nahi ulatna.

§
dono ka matlabfactor lagat (factor cost) = utpadan ki asli lagat — ismein sarkari kar-subsidy nahi jodi jati.
bazaar keemat (market price) = grahak jo chukata hai — ismein kar jud jate hain aur subsidy ghat jati hai.
=
bazaar keemat = factor lagat + apratyaksh kar − subsidy
factor lagat = bazaar keemat − apratyaksh kar + subsidy
sthitiasarkyun
government apratyaksh kar (GST) badhati haibazaar keemat badhti hai, factor lagat wahi rehti haikar grahak deta hai, utpadak ko nahi milta
government subsidy deti haibazaar keemat ghatati haigovernment grahak ki jagah kuch keemat chuka deti hai
kar = subsidydono barabarbazaar keemat = factor lagat
!
Trap — sabse am galatilog kar ko ghatate hain aur subsidy ko jodte hain. sahi hai: bazaar keemat se factor lagat par jane ke liye kar ghatao, subsidy jodo. yaad rakhein — “kar government leti hai (ghatao), subsidy government deti hai (jodo)”.
i
kaun-se kar gine jate hainyahan sirf apratyaksh kar (indirect tax — GST, utpad shulk) jode/ghatae jate hain, kyunki ve keemat mein shamil hote hain. ayakar jaise pratyaksh kar keemat ka hissa nahi hote.
06

anya aay avdharnayeinPersonal income · disposable income · per capita · nominal vs real

vyaktigat aay (Personal Income)
vyakti ko kar se pehle jo aay milti hai. ismein ve chizen nahi judatin jo use milti hi nahi — avitarit korporet labh, korporet kar, samajik suraksha anshadan
prayojy aay (Disposable Income)
Personal Income − pratyaksh kar — yahi vo paisa hai jo “jeb mein aata hai” aur kharch ho sakata hai
per capita income
kul rashtriya aay ÷ kul population — yaani average nagarik ki aay
!
Trap — personal income mein kya nahi jodtekorporet labh company ke malikon/sheyaradharakon ka hota hai, karmachari ka nahi; korporet kar government ka; samajik suraksha anshadan sharton ke baad milta hai. isliye teeno personal income se bahar.

namamatr (Nominal) banam vastavik (Real) aay

prakaarmatlabinflation ka asar
namamatr aaychalu varsh ki prices par mapi gayi aayinflation ka asar dikhata nahi — sankhya badi dikh sakti hai
vastavik aayinflation se samayojit (adjusted) aayasli kray shakti dikhati hai

isliye kaha jata hai — “agar aay 10% badhi aur inflation bhi 10% rahi, to vastavik aay badhi hi nahi.”

✦
ek lain meinNominal = jitna paisa mila · Real = us paise se kitna saman mila.
07

GDP diphletar aur grin GDPGDP deflator · green GDP

§
GDP diphletarye GDP ka upyog karke inflation napta hai.
formula: GDP Deflator = (namamatr GDP ÷ vastavik GDP) × 100.
  • ye poori economy ki prices ka average nikalta hai — sirf consumer vastuon ka nahi.
  • CPI (Consumer Price Index) khudra (retail) prices dekhta hai; GDP diphletar sabhi utpadit vastuon-sevaon ko dekhta hai.
§
grin GDPjab GDP mein paryavaraniya lagat (environmental cost) bhi jod/ghata di jati hai — yaani nature ko hue nukasan ka hisab bhi shamil kiya jata hai.
!
Trapgrin GDP ka matlab “agriculture/Green Revolution wali GDP” nahi hai — ye paryavaraniya lagat ko jodakar nikali gayi GDP hai. aur GDP diphletar inflation napta hai, growth nahi.

09

number kaise bante hain — poora udaharanWorked example: GDP → GNP → NNP → National Income

man low kisi varsh: GDP (bazaar keemat) = ₹1,000, depreciation = ₹100, NFIA = ₹50, apratyaksh kar = ₹80, subsidy = ₹30.

charangannauttar
GDPMPdiya hua1,000
GDPFC1,000 − 80 + 30950
NDPMP1,000 − 100900
GNPMP1,000 + 501,050
NNPMP1,050 − 100950
NNPFC = rashtriya aay950 − 80 + 30900
✦
dekha aapne?GDPFC aur NNPMP dono 950 aaye — yahan sankhyaen jan-bujhakar aisi chuni gayi hain taaki aap apna uttar do tariqe se janch saken. pariksha mein har charan alag likhein, katauti-jod mein gadbad na ho.

panch sabse am jaal

  1. Domestic vs National: seema mein bana = GDP; nagarik ka banaya = GNP.
  2. Gross vs Net: depreciation ghatao to Net.
  3. Market price vs Factor cost: kar ghatao, subsidy jodo.
  4. Transfer income: penshan/chhatravritti rashtriya aay mein nahi.
  5. Final vs intermediate: faisla upyog se hota hai, vastu se nahi.
★
pariksha pattern“NNP at factor cost kise kehte hain?” → rashtriya aay. “GDP + NFIA = ?” → GNP. “GDP − depreciation = ?” → NDP. teeno seedhe ek-lain question hain.
