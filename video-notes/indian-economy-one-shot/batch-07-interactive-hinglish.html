01

fiscal policy kya hai?Fiscal policy: taxation, expenditure, debt

lecture ki sabse sidhi paribhasha — “fiscal policy ka simple sa matlab hai taxation.” jaise RBI ki monetary policy hai, waise government ki fiscal policy hai.

§
Fiscal Policygovernment ki ve policies jo public expenditure, taxation, public debt aur deficit financing se judi hon. government kar ke zariye bazaar mein paisa badhati-ghatati hai.
pahaluFiscal policyMonetary policy
kiski policy?government (finance Ministry)RBI
mukhya toolkar, sarvajanik kharch, udharirepo, CRR, SLR, OMO
sidha asarmang aur aay parpaise ki apurti aur byaaj rate par
§
fiscal policy ke maksad (lecture ke shabdon mein) 1. sansadhanon ko achhe se jutana (resource mobilisation) · 2. sansadhanon ka efficient allocation · 3. price stability aur inflation par niyantran · 4. employment paida karna · 5. balanced regional development — kahin ek hi ilaqa developed na ho jaaye · 6. capital formation.
!
Trap — dono policies ka malik kaunFiscal policy = government, Monetary policy = RBI. question mein “RBI ki fiscal policy” likhakar jaal bichhaya jata hai — RBI ki policy monetary hi hoti hai.
✦
yaad rakhne ka tariqaFiscal = Finance Ministry (government) · Monetary = Money (RBI). dono ek hi target ki taraf — inflation kaabu mein, growth ruke nahi.
02

FRBM Act 2003 aur deficits ke prakaarFiscal discipline & deficits

2003 se pehle government bahut udhar le rahi thi. us par lagam lagane ke liye FRBM Act aaya — aur yahin se deficits (deficit) ke sawal shuru hote hain.

§
FRBM Act, 2003Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act — iska kanooni infrastructure fiscal deficit ko niyantrit karne ke liye bana. lecture mein kaha gaya: “fiscal deficit ka target rakha — ye GDP ke 3% se zyada nahi hona chahiye.”

i
video ke bahar se, par pariksha mein aata haideficits chaar tarah ke poochhe jate hain — Fiscal deficit = kul kharch − kul aay (udhar chhodakar) · Revenue deficit = revenue kharch − revenue aay · Primary deficit = fiscal deficit − byaaj payment · Effective revenue deficit = revenue deficit − states ko diye capital grants. lecture mein sirf “fiscal deficit = budget wali video mein” kahakar chhoda gaya hai.
!
Trap — 3% aur 60% mein ulajhna nahi3% = fiscal deficit ki seema (FRBM 2003) · 60% = debt-to-GDP ka sujhav (NK Singh 2016). dono alag chizen hain aur alag samitiyon se judi hain.

budget — constitutional provision aur pehla budgetBudget: Article 112 / 202

lecture mein budget ki do hi baatein zor se boli gain — Article 112 (Centre) aur Article 202 (rajya), aur 1947 ka pehla budget.

questionuttar

rajya ka budget kis Article mein?Article 202
svatantr Bharat ka pehla budgetR. K. Shanmukham Chetty — 26 November 1947
i
video ke bahar se, par pariksha mein aata haiRailway budget ko 2017 mein am budget mein mila diya gaya. budget ke saath aane wale document — Finance Bill (kar proposal), Appropriation Bill (kharch ki approval), aur Macro Economic Framework Statement. 1 February ko budget pesh karne ki tradition 2017 se shuru hui (pehle February ke akhir mein).
§
budget kitne tarah ka hota hai?Balanced budget — aay = kharch · Surplus budget — aay zyada · Deficit budget — kharch zyada. Bharat jaise developing desh mein am taur par deficit budget hi rehta hai, kyunki development ke liye udhar lena padta hai.
§
budget ke do hisseRevenue budget — kar aur gair-kar aay, aur vetan-subsidy jaisa roz ka kharch. Capital budget — udhar, capital receipts aur sadak-bandh jaise capital expenditure. deficits ki ginti inhi do hisson se hoti hai.
✦
ek lain mein112 = Centre · 202 = rajya · 1947 = pehla budget (Chetty). teen tarikhen-Article, ek hi sans mein yaad kar lein.

direct kar (Direct Taxes)Income · Corporation · Capital gains

“Direct tax vo jo aap seedhe government ko dete ho.” lecture mein inki poori List boli gayi — aur saath mein ek bada apavad bhi.

§
Direct Taxjo kar seedhe government ko chukaya jata hai aur jiska bhar tala nahi ja sakata — jaise income tax, corporation tax. Bharat mein direct tax bahut kam log dete hain.

Trap — agriculture aay par income tax nahi“Agriculture par koi bhi income tax nahi lagata” — lecture ki saaf lain. par dhyan dein: agriculture aay par Centre income tax nahi leta; is par kar lagane ka adhikar rajya government ke pass hai (aur vyavahar mein lagbhag sabhi states ne chhoot di hai).
✎
reform — jo kar ab hat chuke hainlecture mein teen kar “abolish” batae gaye — Expenditure Tax, Estate Duty (uttaradhikar par) aur Wealth Tax. yaad rakhein: wealth tax 2015-16 se samapt kar diya gaya aur uski jagah super-rich par surcharge lagaya gaya. pariksha mein “kaun ab nahi hai” poocha jaaye to yahi teen.
✦
yaad rakhne ka tariqaDirect tax = “jiska bojh usi par” — jo kamata hai wahi deta hai. Indirect tax = “jo kharidta hai wahi deta hai” — isliye ise “gareeb ka tax” bhi kehte hain.

§
Ad valorem banam Specific dutyAd valorem — value (qimat) ke hisab se lagane wala kar (jaise 18% GST). Specific duty — quantity ke hisab se, jaise prati tan ya prati litar.
§
VAT — Value Added Taxkar sirf jodi gayi qimat par. lecture ka udaharan: ₹100 ka bred kharida aur ₹200 mein becha — value addition ₹100 hua, VAT isi ₹100 par. ye pehli baar frans mein aaya; Bharat mein sabse pehle Haryana (2003) aur uttar pradesh mein 2008 mein implemented hua.
!
Trap — VAT aur GST ek nahiVAT rajya star ka kar tha aur sirf goods par lagata tha. GST uska bada roop hai — pure desh mein ek, aur vastu + seva dono par. question mein “VAT kahan pehli baar implemented” poochhe to Haryana, “GST kab implemented” poochhe to 1 July 2017.

kar ki teen shaklen — regressive, proportional, progressiveTax structures · PAN · Foreign Trade Policy 2023

kar ka naam nahi, uska infrastructure dekhie — aay badhane par rate badhti hai, ghatati hai, ya wahi rehti hai? isi se teeno naam tay hote hain.

kismaay badhane par ratekis par bhar zyada
Regressiveghatati haigarib par zyada
Proportionalwahi rehti hai (flat tax)sab par barabar
Progressivebadhti haiamir par zyada
§
Regressive tax“Tax rate decrease ho jaaye as income increases” — yaani jaise-jaise aay badhe, rate kam ho jaaye. isse bojh garib par zyada padta hai. Proportional mein flat rate rehta hai aur progressive mein aay badhane par rate bhi badhti hai.
§
PAN — Permanent Account NumberIncome Tax Department jari karta hai. card par naam, date of birth, PAN number aur signature hote hain. bank khata, ITR, badi kharid — sab mein yahi pehchan hai.
§
videsh trade policy (Foreign Trade Policy) 202331 March 2023 ko piyush goyal ji ne launch ki. target — 2030 tak 2 triliyan dolar ka export. chaar stanbh: 1. incentives dena · 2. export promotion · 3. collaboration aur ease of doing business · 4. emerging areas mein export ke opportunities banana.
!
Trap — regressive ka ulta nahi samajhenRegressive kar mein rate garib par kam nahi, balki bojh zyada hota hai (kyunki uski aay ka bada hissa kharch hota hai). question mein “regressive = amir par zyada bhar” likha ho to vo galat hai — vo progressive hai.
✦
yaad rakhne ka tariqaRegressive = Reverse (aay badhi, rate ghati) · Proportional = Plain/flat · Progressive = Progress (aay badhi, rate badhi).
