yojana kya hai?Meaning & origin of economic planning

batch 2 poora yojana (Planning) chapter hai — UPPCS aur BPSC dono ke liye sabse zyada scoring hissa, kyunki yahan se sawal seedhe period, model aur naam par bante hain.

§
paribhashaeconomic planning = pehle se tay kiye gaye objectives (predetermined objectives) ko, uplabdh sansadhanon ke ishtatam upyog (optimum use of existing resources) se prapt karne ki koshish.

lecture ki sidhi baat: “jaise aap padhne se pehle apna plan banate ho aur use achieve karne ki koshish karte ho — wahi cheez yahan bhi hai.”

reform / sateektalecture mein ek jagah “12 panchvarshiya + 6 annual yojanayein” bola gaya, par baad mein khud hi ginti ki — 3 annual (1966–69) + 2 annual (1990–92) = kul 5. pariksha mein 5 annual yojanayein hi sahi mani jati hain.

yojana ka constitutional sanket

  • Directive Principles (DPSP): Constitution ke bhaag IV mein bhi planning ki baat hai.
  • adhikar kahan se: “yojana” vishay Concurrent List mein hai — yaani is par law Centre bhi bana sakata hai aur rajya bhi.
  • anusmriti: Seventh Schedule mein teen suchiyan — Union List, State List, Concurrent List.

03

Planning Commission aur National Development CouncilPlanning Commission & NDC

establishment ki steps

  1. 1947: Congress (AICC) ne economic karyakram committee banai — chairman Nehru.
  2. 25 January 1948: recommendation — ek permanent Planning Commission hona chahiye.
  3. 26 January 1950: Constitution implemented hua.
  4. 15 March 1950: Planning Commission ki establishment — Cabinet proposal (Cabinet Resolution) se, recommendation Niyogi Committee ki.
  5. 1 April 1951: pehli panchvarshiya yojana shuru.
establishment
15 March 1950 · Cabinet proposal
recommendation
Niyogi Committee
first chairman
Jawaharlal Nehru (ex-officio — PM)
first Vice-Chairman
Gulzarilal Nanda

Planning Commission ki nature

  • na constitutional, na statutory — ye executive body tha, Cabinet proposal se bana.
  • advisory body — keval salah deta tha; headquarters New Delhi.
  • atyadhik centralised — members mukhyatah Centre ke; states ki bhoomika kam.
  • Vice-Chairman ko President appointed karte the — Cabinet Minister star, Prime Minister ki salah par, 5 varsh ke liye.
  • mukhya kaarya: jeevan-star improve, utpadan badhana, sansadhanon ka kushal upyog, employment ke opportunities paida karna.
  • 2015 mein dissolved → NITI Aayog ne replaced kiya.

National Development Council (NDC)

6 August 1952
NDC ki establishment (Niyogi Committee ki recommendation)
PM
NDC ka chairman — Prime Minister
Super Cabinet
vistrit membership aur adhikar ke kaaran
  • members: sabhi kendriya Minister, sabhi states ke Chief Minister, sabhi Union Territory states ke administrator.
  • ex-officio members: Planning Commission ke sabhi members svatah NDC ke members.
  • Secretary: Planning Commission ka Secretary hi NDC ka Secretary.
  • kaarya: states aur Commission ke beech cooperative planning, policy-nirman, yojana par charcha aur antim approval.
  • nature: ye bhi gair-constitutional body hai.
!
Trap — do tarikhen15 March 1950 = Planning Commission · 6 August 1952 = NDC. dono ki recommendation Niyogi Committee ne ki — par tarikh badalane par uttar galat ho jaayega.
04

pehli aur doosri yojanaFirst & Second Five Year Plan · Harrod-Domar & Mahalanobis

pehli yojana (1951–56)

  • objective: agriculture development, sharanarthiyon ka rehabilitation (refugee rehabilitation), balanced development.
  • target: 2.1% growth rate — target se zyada prapt (achieved/ exceeded).
  • model: Harrod–Domar model (Harrod-Domar).
§
Harrod–Domar modeldo alag model — sir Roy Harrod (UK) aur evsi Domar (USA) — milakar ye model bana. mool baat: saving aur investment ka growth par sidha prabhav.
chakra: saving → bank mein jama → industrialists ko rin → factory → employment → vetan → maang → utpadan… (ek vicious cycle jo growth mein badalata hai)

doosri yojana (1956–61)

  • focus: bade measure par industrialisation, visheshakar bhari industry; rashtriya aay mein 25% growth ka target.
  • target 4.5% → prapt 4.3% — target poora nahi hua.
  • model: P.C. Mahalanobis model.
kaun the
P.C. Mahalanobis — Bharatiya statistician (Indian statistician)
inka yogdan
doosri yojana ka blueprint prepare kiya
mool vichar
tez industrialisation + import substitution

sectorkya aata haiobjective

i
import substitution ka matlabjo cheez bahar se mangate hain (import), uska apne desh mein vikalp (substitute) bana do — taaki bahar par nirbharta ghate aur economy self-reliant bane.
!
Trap — model ki jodipehli = Harrod–Domar, doosri = Mahalanobis. pariksha mein ulat-pher kar poocha jata hai — “Mahalanobis model kis yojana mein?” → doosri.

tisri yojana aur Plan HolidayThird plan · wars · Plan Holiday

  • period: 1961–66 · target: 5% annual rashtriya aay growth + self-reliance.
  • aadhar: Mahalanobis model · SDS pradarshan model · Sukhamoy Chakravarty ka input-output model.
  • asli dikkat: 25% jaisa bada target nae desh ke liye lagbhag asanbhav — “ek tarah se prayog (experiment) chal raha tha”.
varshghatnaasar
1962Bharat–chin yuddhresources raksha ki or chale gaye
1965Bharat–pakistan yuddhyojana ke resources aur simit

Plan Holiday (1966–69)

  • yuddhon ke kaaran sansadhanon ki bhari kami → koi panchvarshiya yojana nahi bani.
  • isi samay Green Revolution (Green Revolution, 1966) shuru hui.
  • isliye teen annual yojanayein banin — jab long-term mein kaam na ho, to short-term mein sochate hain.
✦
yaad rakhne ka kram1951–56 → 1956–61 → 1961–66 → teen annual (1966–69) → 1969–74 → 1974–79 → …
!
Trap“Plan Holiday” ka matlab yojana band hona nahi — panchvarshiya yojana nahi bani, par annual yojanayein chalati rahin.
06

chauthi aur panchavin yojanaFourth (Gadgil) & Fifth (Garibi Hatao)

chauthi yojana (1969–74)

  • model: Gadgil yojana (Gadgil Plan) — direct question banta hai: “kaun-si yojana Gadgil yojana par aadharit thi?” → chauthi.
  • nayi concept: buffer stock — khadyann ka surakshit bhandar.
  • bada kadam: banks ka nationalisation (bank nationalisation).
  • target poora nahi hua.

panchavin yojana (1974–79)

  • slogan: Garibi Hatao.
  • karyakram: Minimum Needs Programme, Food for Work.
  • parinaam: target 4.4% → achievement 4.8% — saphal yojana.
  • adhuri rahi: Janata Party ke satta mein aane par yojana samay se pehle samapt (terminate) kar di gayi.
!
Trap — alochna“Garibi Hatao” ki alochna bhi hui — ki slogan poverty hatane ka tha, par vyavahar mein garibon ko hi hataya ja raha hai. ye famous satire pariksha mein vikalp ke roop mein aa sakata hai.
07

chhathi-satavin yojana aur Rolling PlanSixth, Seventh & Rolling Plan

  • chhathi yojana (1980–85): poverty unmulan + modernisation; yojana kaafi had tak saphal rahi.
  • satavin yojana (1985–90): modernisation, self-reliance; yahin se liberalisation ka model shuru hua.
  • satavin mein yojanaon par zor — Indira Awaas yojana, Jawahar employment yojana; target prapt hua.
  • satavin ke baad do annual yojanayein (1990–92) — isliye kul annual yojanayein 3 + 2 = 5.
§
Rolling Plan (Rolling Plan, 1978–83)credit: Gunnar Myrdal. Bharat mein lane wali government: Morarji Desai ki Janata Party government. us samay finance Minister: H.M. Patel.
vichar: har saal yojana ko aage badhate jana — teen-chaar saal ka target tay kar, agle saal use sanshodhit karna.
i
Perspective Planningchhathi yojana mein long-term pariprekshy planning (Perspective Planning) ka vichar istemal hua — 15–20 saal ki disha tay karke 5 saal ki yojana banana.
!
Trap — Rolling Plan ka “credit”vichar Gunnar Myrdal ka, Bharat mein implemented Morarji Desai government ne (1978–83). dono ko ek hi question mein ulajhaya jata hai.

athavin se barahavin yojanaEighth to Twelfth Plan (1992–2017)

i
athavin yojana ka modelJohn W. Miller model (John W. Miller model) par aadharit.

nauvin yojana ke 7 Basic Minimum Services

  • primary education · begharon ke liye Awaas · poshan (nutrition) hetu khadyann
  • gramin roads · surakshit drinking water · vaccination/health suvidha · mazboot PDS
!
Trap — barahavin yojanaye 2012–17 ke liye bani thi, par 2015 mein NITI Aayog ban jane ke kaaran poori nahi ho saki — yahi sabse zyada poocha jane wala bindu hai.
✦
