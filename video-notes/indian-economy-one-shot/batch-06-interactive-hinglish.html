06

money supply M1–M4, CRR aur SLRMoney supply measures & reserve ratios

₹100 bank mein aaya — usmein se kitna RBI ke pass jaayega, kitna bank apne pass rakhega aur kitna loan ke liye bachega? yahi CRR aur SLR ka poora khel hai.

§
CRR — Cash Reserve Ratio (lagbhag 4%)bank mein deposit ₹100 mein se 4% yaani ₹4 bank ko RBI ke pass naqad (cash) rakhna padta hai. is ₹4 par bank ko koi byaaj nahi milta.
§
SLR — Statutory Liquidity Ratio (lagbhag 18%)₹100 mein se 18% yaani ₹18 bank ko apne pass hi alag rakhna hota hai (sarkari securities/sone ke roop mein). ye paisa RBI ke pass nahi jata, aur is par bank ko byaaj bhi milta hai.
₹100 ki depositraqamkahan rehti haibyaaj
CRR 4%₹4RBI ke pass, naqadnahi
SLR ~18%₹18bank ke pass hihan
Loan ke liye bacha₹78bazaar meinbank kamata hai
§
money supply ke chaar maap M1 = Janata ke pass karensi + net demand deposits (jo kabhi bhi nikale ja saken) + RBI ke pass anya deposit — sabse zyada taral
M2 = M1 + dakaghar ki saving deposit
M3 = M1 + banks ki fixed deposits — ise broad money kehte hain
M4 = M3 + dakaghar ki deposit (kul)
i
lecture ki salah“M1–M4 ratane ki zarurat nahi, shayad nahi poocha jaaye” — par M1 = narrow money aur M3 = broad money ye do baatein pariksha mein seedhe aati hain. M1 mein karensi + demand deposit hote hain, FD nahi.
!
Trap — CRR kiske pass, SLR kiske passCRR = RBI ke pass · SLR = bank ke apne pass. yahi ek lain sabse zyada galat hoti hai. yaad rakhein: “jo RBI ke pass jata hai vo CRR (Central bank), jo apne pass rehta hai vo SLR (Self).”
✦
RBI daren badalati kyun hai?agar RBI chahti hai ki bazaar mein zyada paisa jaaye, to SLR ₹18 se ghatakar 15% kar degi — tab bank ke pass loan dene ke liye ₹82 bachenge (₹78 ki jagah). paisa badhana hai to daren ghatao, paisa ghatana hai to daren badhao.
07

monetary policy ke tool — repo, reverse repo, OMOMonetary policy instruments & objectives

monetary policy ka maksad ek hi hai — paise ki quantity ko aisa rakhna ki inflation kaabu mein rahe aur growth ruke nahi. RBI ke pass iske liye kai tool hain; har tool ka “tera-mera” samajh lein.

§
repo ratemaan low Kotak Mahindra Bank ko paise ki zarurat padi. vo RBI ke pass jaayega aur apni sarkari securities (government securities) girvi rakh dega; RBI badale mein paisa de dega. Repo = Repurchase Agreement — bank ko bhavishya mein paisa lautakar apni securities vapas leni hoti hain.
§
reverse repoulti sthiti — banks ke pass zyada paisa hai, to ve use RBI ke pass park kar dete hain; RBI unhen sarkari security deti hai aur byaaj deti hai. reverse repo rate repo se kam hoti hai aur iska kaam extra liquidity sokhna hai.
toolkya hota haikiske liye
RepoRBI bank ko security ke badale paisa deti haibank ke pass paisa kam ho to
Reverse repoRBI bank se paisa lekar security deti haibazaar mein paisa zyada ho to
MSFSLR ki securities girvi rakhakar loanjab aur security bachi na ho
Bank ratepehle discounting ke liye, ab penalty rateshart poori na karne par jurmana
OMORBI khula bazaar mein security kharidti/bechati haipaisa badhana ya ghatana
§
OMO — Open Market OperationRBI chahti hai ki bazaar mein paisa badhe (kyunki logon ke pass paisa nahi, mang nahi badh rahi) — to vo sarkari securities kharid legi aur bazaar mein paisa chhod degi. paisa ghatana hai to apni securities bech degi — log paisa denge, liquidity sukh jaegi.
§
Quantitative banam QualitativeQuantitative — paise ki quantity seedhe niyantrit: bank rate, MSF, reverse repo, repo, CRR, SLR, OMO.
Qualitative — credit ka bahav kisi khas sector ki or modna: “bhai, itna paisa to is sector ko dena hi dena hai” — margin requirement, credit rationing, moral suasion.
i
policy kab ghoshit hoti hai?RBI saal mein 6 baar (har 2 mahine mein) monetary policy ki ghoshna karta hai — repo kitna, reverse repo kitna. faisla inflation/deflation ki sthiti dekhakar hota hai.
i
video ke bahar se, par pariksha mein aata haiye faisla monetary policy committee (MPC) leti hai — kul 6 members (3 RBI ke, 3 government dwara namit), chairman RBI Governor. target 4% ± 2% — batch 5 mein yahi vistar se padha tha.
✦
ek lain mein poora tantrinflation badhi → RBI repo/CRR/SLR badhati hai → loan mahange → mang ghatati → inflation ghatati. susti ai → ulta karti hai.

inflation ka asar aur depreciationWho gains, who loses & devaluation vs depreciation

inflation sabake liye ek jaisi nahi hoti — koi kamata hai, koi harta hai. aur karensi ke price mein badalav seedhe export–import se juda hai.

kauninflation meindeflation mein

lendernuqasan — lautaya paisa kam qimat kafayda
debtorfayda — kam qimat chukakar qarz utaranuqasan
sthir aay wala (fixed income)nuqasan — aay wahi, prices badhinfayda
§
Lender vs debtor — lecture ka chaval wala udaharanaj ₹100 udhar diye aur us vaqt ₹100 mein 2 kilo chaval aata tha. jab paisa vapas mila tab chaval ₹70/kilo ho gaya — ab usi ₹100 mein 2 kilo nahi aenge. yaani jisne paisa diya (lender) uska nuqasan, aur jisne liya (debtor) use fayda.
§
depreciation (Depreciation / Devaluation)ek pen Bharat mein ₹10 ka hai aur $1 = ₹50. tab USA wala ₹50 mein 5 pen kharidta tha. ab dolar ₹80 ka ho gaya — wahi ₹50? nahi, wahi $1 ab 8 pen kharidega. yaani Bharat ka export badh jaayega. bazaar ki taqaton se rupya gire = depreciation; government/RBI janabujhakar girae = devaluation.
§
appreciationulta — dolar ₹50 se ₹40 ka ho gaya to USA wala 4 pen hi kharidega: export ghatega. aur import sasta ho jaayega — pehle $1 mein ek bairal tel ₹50 mein milta tha, ab ₹40 mein milega.
sthitiExportImport
$1 = ₹50 → ₹80 (depreciation)badhta haimahanga hota hai
$1 = ₹50 → ₹40 (appreciation)ghatata haisasta hota hai
✎
reform — Bharat mein depreciation ke varshlecture mein “1949, 1969, 1991” bola gaya. sahi kram 1949, 1966 aur 1991 hai — 1966 mein rupye ka bada devaluation hua tha (1969 nahi). 1991 wala devaluation LPG sudharon ke saath juda hai.
!
Trap — depreciation banam devaluationdono mein karensi ki qimat ghatati hai. farq kaaran ka hai: depreciation bazaar se apne aap, devaluation government/RBI ke faisle se. question mein “market forces” shabd aaye to depreciation.
