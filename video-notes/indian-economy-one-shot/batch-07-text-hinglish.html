1 · fiscal policy aur FRBMFiscal policy

ab hum log baat karte hain fiscal policy ki. dekho, fiscal policy ka simple sa matlab hai taxation. matlab ki government jo hai na tax ke through market mein paisa badhati-ghatati hai. jaise ki RBI ka monetary policy hai, to government ka fiscal policy hai.

fiscal policy kya hai? ye government ki policies hoti hain — public eksapendichar se related, taxation se related, public dept aur dephisit phainensing se related.

parpas kya hai? wahi — resource ko mobilaij karna achhe tareeke se, resource ka efficient elokeshan karna, prais stebiliti aur inflation ko control karna, rozgar paida karna, bailensd regional development karna — aisa na ho ki ek hi eriya zyada develop ho jaaye aur baki na ho — aur capital formation karna.

ab kya ho raha tha na bhai, 2003 se pehle kaafi zyada government udhar le rahi thi. to usko regulet karne ke liye ephaarabiem — fiscal rispansibiliti end budget management Act — aata hai, 2003 wala. iska ligal framework tha fiscal dephisit ko control karna. fiscal dephisit kya hota hai? budget wali video dekh lena.

fiscal dephisit ka inhone target rakha — it shud not be more den 3% of GDP. aur phir ephaarabiem ki ek rivyu kamiti banani thi, 2016 mein en ke sinh — unhone sajest kiya ki jo dept to GDP ratio hai na vo 60% se zyada nahi hona chahiye. central government ka 40%, State ka 20%. aur inhone bola ki ek indipendent fiscal Council bhi banai jaaye taaki is cheez ko regulet kiya ja sake.

2 · budget — Article aur pehla budgetBudget

public Finance aur budget vee haiv diskasd in veri veri diteld. State budget Article 202 mein hota hai, Union budget Article 112 mein hota hai. pehla budget jo indipendent India ka, vo diya tha ar ke sanmukham chetti ne. to ye aapko yaad rakhna hai.

to ye tha guys hamara ye wala chapter. ab hum log aage badhte hain, jo hai hamara fiscal policy — dekho budget ka poora infrastructure, revenyu budget, capital budget, deficits ke prakaar — ye sab humne ditel mein kiya hua hai, yahan ek doharav bhar hai.

3 · direct karDirect taxes

ab direct tax kya hote hain? jo aap dayarektali government ko dete ho. jaise income tax, koraporeshan tax, Minimum tax, velth tax, State dyuti, capital gen tax.

dekho, direct tax to bahut kam log dete hain Bharat mein. lekin jo inadayarekt tax hai na vo almost har koi de raha hai — har koi. aap koi bhi product agar market se kharidte ho, agar aap ji rahe ho Bharat mein, to aap tax de hi rahe ho inadayarekt wala.

income tax ye central government kalekt karti hai, lekin agriculture pe koi bhi income tax nahi lagata — ye aapko yaad rakhna hai. koraporeshan tax kya hai? ek company hai, uske profit pe tax lagata hai — korporet tax; isko bhi central government hi kalekt karti hai.

next hai eksapendichar tax — ye direct kanjampashan pe lagata tha, ab ye abolish kiya ja chuka hai. estet dyuti kya hai? inaheritens pe kuch tax lagata hai — ki aapne apne purvajon se kuch proparti lee hai to us pe kuch tax lagega. ye bhi abolish kar diya gaya hai. velth tax kya hai? aapki net velth pe lagata tha — ye bhi abolish ho gaya.

4 · indirect kar, VAT aur GSTIndirect taxes

inadayarekt tax kya hota hai? jo aap goods aur services pe dete ho. example ke taur pe, agar aap ek maigi ka paiket bhi khareed rahe ho to bhi aap tax de rahe ho.

service tax kya hai? 1994-95 mein introdyus kiya gaya tha — aap koi bhi service lete ho to us pe tax lagata hai. central eksaij dyuti kya hai? eksaij ye production of goods pe lagata hai — aapne koi bhi cheez produce ki hai Bharat mein to uske production pe aapko tax dena padega. kastam dyuti kya hai? agar aap kuch bhi import ya export karte ho to aapko kastam dyuti deni padati hai.

add veloram tax jo hai vo value pe lagata hai, aur spesiphik dyuti lagata hai kvantiti pe — aap kitni kvantiti paraches kar rahe ho.

vet kya hota hai? value edishan tax, of value edishan pe. let's se aapne ₹100 ka bred kharida aur usko phir baad mein jaake ₹200 ka bech diya. ye aapka intaramidiet gud hai — matlab overall aapka itna hi kharcha hai intaramidiet gud ka. to aapki value edishan ho jaegi — final product mainas intaramidiet gud iz equal to ₹100. yaani ki ₹100 ka aapne value edishan kiya.

aur pehli baar jo vet hai vo France mein aaya tha, aur India mein pehli baar aaya tha Haryana mein 2003 mein. UP mein aaya tha 2008 mein. to ye yaad rakhna hai.

GST kya hai? goods end services tax kab launch kiya gaya? 1 July 2017 ko, 101 Constitution amendament Act ke dwara. ye ek singal tax hai jo ki goods end services ki supply pe lagaya jata hai. ab ismein sijiesti sentar ke dwara, esajiesti State, aijiesti intarastet tred se related — alag-alag tax slab hain: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%.

aapko itna yaad rakhna hai — alkohal pe aur dijal, ATF, petroleum prodakts pe GST nahi lagata.

5 · kar ke prakaar, PAN aur videsh trade policyTax structures, PAN & FTP

type of tax — rigresiv, matlab bardan on poor jo hai na vo zyada rehta hai. tax rate inakriz-dikriz ho jaaye aiz income inakrizes. isko rigresiv tax bolte hain — yaani ki jaise aapki income badhegi na to jo tax rate hai na vo aap pe kam ho jaayega. proporshanal — phlait tax rate, ki agar income badhegi to tax bhi badhega. progresiv tax bhi sem hi hota hai.

pain number yaani permanent akaunt number — pain card to ye ishyu kiya jata hai income tax department ke dwara. ismein aapka naam, date of barth, pain number, signechar — ye saari cheezein hoti hain.

phoren tred policy kya hai bhai? ye 31 March 2023 ko launch ki gayi thi hamare Minister piyush goyal ji ke dwara. aur inka goal kya tha? ki 2 triliyan tak ka humein India ko export karna hai, 2030 tak.

iske chaar pilar hain — insentivaiz karna, export promote karna, kolaiboreshan ke through dusaron ke saath iz of duing promote karna (iz of duing bizanes), aur imarjing eriya mein export ki aporchunitiz ko develop karna.

to ye saari cheezein aap log yaad rakhna — yahan se direct fact based question aate hain.
