01

poverty kya hai?Poverty — meaning, types & poverty line

ye chapter poori series ka sabse zyada question-kari hissa hai — “kaun-si committee, kis saal, kitne percent” yahin se seedhe poocha jata hai.

§
paribhashapoverty = aisi sthiti jismein vyakti apni basic needs (basic necessities) poori nahi kar pata — roti, kapada, makan yaani food, clothing, shelter.
i
sabse pehla vaishvik estimateduniya mein poverty ka pehla global estimate World Development Report 1990 mein aaya — “varld bank ne pehli baar gina ki kitne log garib hain.”

do tarah ki poverty

prakaarmatlabkaise mapi jati hai
relative poverty
Relative		 tulna mein garib — “padosi ke pass badi gadi hai, to aap khud ko garib mahasus karte hain.” ye asamanta (disparity) napti hai, aay-samuhon ke beech ka fasla. Lorenz curve aur gini coefficient
absolute poverty
Absolute		 ek nyoonatam zarurat ka measure — agar roz itna paisa kharch nahi kar pa rahe, ya itni calorie nahi le pa rahe, to aap garib hain. ismein tulna nahi hoti. poverty line — minimum consumption criteria
!
TrapBharat mein absolute vidhi istemal hoti hai, relative nahi. aur poverty line = “nyoonatam upbhog ka measure” — yahi absolute poverty ka maap hai.

02

asamanta kaise mapte hainInequality — Lorenz curve, Gini coefficient & Amartya Sen

  • Lorenz curve: aay/asset ke vitaran mein asamanta dikhata hai — population ka kitna hissa, kul aay ka kitna hissa pa raha hai.
  • gini coefficient: Lorenz curve se nikla 0 se 1 ke beech ka ek number. 0 = poorn samanta, 1 = poorn asamanta.
  • dono ka kaam ek hi: aay-asamanta (income inequality) mapna — ye baar-baar poocha jata hai.
§
Amartya Sen ka yogdanInequality-adjusted per capita income — average per capita income ko asamanta ke hisab se theek karke dekhna.
vichar: “average aay badh jana kaafi nahi — agar aay barabar banti nahi hai, to vo average dhokha deta hai.”
per capita income
kul rashtriya aay ÷ kul population
kami
ye average hai — amir-garib ka farq chhipa jata hai
samadhan
Amartya Sen ka inequality-adjusted maap
★
pariksha pattern“Lorenz curve / gini coefficient kisse sanbandhit hain?” → aay-asamanta. “Inequality-adjusted per capita income kisne diya?” → Amartya Sen.

manaregaMGNREGA — the legal guarantee of work

shuruat
2 February 2006 — pehle 200 districts mein, Andhra Pradesh se
phailav
2007-08 mein 130 aur district → 2008 se pure desh mein (jammu-kashmir ko chhodakar)
naam
2009 mein “mahatma Gandhi” joda gaya — pehle sirf NREGA thi
law
NREGA Act 2005 ke tahat — ye legal adhikar hai, keval yojana nahi

mukhya pravadhan

  • 100 din ka guaranteed unskilled employment, har gramin family ke ek vayask members ko.
  • avedan ke 15 din ke bhitar kaam na mile to unemployment bhatta (unemployment allowance) milega.
  • kaam 5 kilometre ke dayre mein; isse door ho to wage mein 10% extra (yatra/rehne ka kharch).
  • kam-se-kam 1/3 labharthi women honi chahiye.
  • wage rate rajya-var notified, inflation se judi (indexed).
kharch ka hissakaun uthata hai
unskilled shram ki wageCentre — 100%
material aur kushal/ardh-kushal wageCentre 75%, rajya 25%
prashasnik kharchCentre 6%
kaam ka distributionshram : material = 60 : 40
!
Trap“NREGA 2005” aur “MGNREGA 2006” dono sahi dikhate hain — law 2005 ka, shuruat 2 February 2006 se, aur naam mein “mahatma Gandhi” 2009 mein juda. question kis cheez ka poocha gaya hai, vo dekhen.
08

ajivika mishan aur startaapNRLM · NULM · Startup India · Stand Up India · Skill

ganv ki ajivika — DAY-NRLM

  • SGSY (1999) ka naya roop → National Rural Livelihood Mission (NRLM), 2011 → 2015 mein naam badalakar Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – NRLM.
  • target: BPL families ka swarozgar aur aay srijan, SHG ke zarie.
  • labharthi: 50% SC/ST, 15% alpasankhyak, 3% divyang.
  • phanding: Centre : rajya = 75 : 25 → ye Centre sponsored (centrally sponsored) yojana hai (kendriya sector yojana mein 100% Centre deta hai).

shahar ki ajivika — DAY-NULM

  • 2013 mein shuru; isne SJSRY (1997) ko badala. poora naam: Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Urban Livelihood Mission.
  • un sabhi districts mein implemented jahan 2011 ki census ke anusar 1 lakh se zyada abadi hai.
  • phanding wahi 75 : 25 (Centre : rajya); mantralay — Awaas evam shahari poverty unmulan mantralay.
!
Trap — kram ulta haiSJSRY (shahar, 1997) → SGSY (ganv, 1999): pehle shahar. par NRLM (ganv, 2011) → NULM (shahar, 2013): pehle ganv. dono kram alag-alag yaad rakhein.

startaap, staind up aur kaushal

ghoshna 15 August 2015, launch 16 January 2016 — yuvaon ko swarozgar aur nae udyam ke liye protsahan
staind up India
ghoshna 15 August 2015, launch 5 April 2016 — SC/ST aur women mein udyamashilta; ₹10 lakh–₹1 crore ka rin
rashtriya kaushal policy
2015 — skill India mishan ke antargat
PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana
2015 — target: 2022 tak 40 crore logon ko prashikshan
✦
